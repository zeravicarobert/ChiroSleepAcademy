# Liberpulco 2026 — Attendee Info Sheet

A clean, scannable rundown for ticket holders, organized from the official
Anarchapulco / Liberland attendee email. Schedules update regularly — check
the official links before relying on times.

---

## Where & When

- **Venue:** ARK Liberland Village, Mirni Dunav 3, Apatin 25260, Serbia
- **Main event:** August 12–15, 2026
- **Bonus Liberland excursion day:** August 16, 2026
- **Floating Man extension:** August 17–23, 2026 (sold separately)

## Day by Day

| Date | What's happening |

## What Your Liberpulco Ticket Includes

- **Every in-person tier:** virtual access + lifetime replay
- **VIP and above:** food and drinks
- **VIP+ tiers:** accommodation Aug 11–17 (private suite at Barcode Garni
  Hotel, Sombor — daily breakfast + wellness area access)
- **Signature and Merit tiers:** Liberland citizenship
- **No tier includes flights** — arrange travel to Serbia separately
- **Floating Man is excluded** unless purchased before June 15th (if you have
  access you've received an email; if unsure, contact
  `helpdesk@anarchapulco.com`)

## Getting Around (Shuttles)

- **Daily Shuttles ($50):** Aug 12–15, within Sombor and Apatin
- **Belgrade ↔ Liberpulco Shuttles ($40):** Belgrade → Apatin on the 11th or
  12th, and back on the 16th or 17th
- **Both included in VIP and VIP+ tiers**

## Where to Stay

- **VIP+ tiers:** private suite at **Barcode Garni Hotel, Sombor**, Aug 11–17
  — daily breakfast + wellness area (pool, sauna, steam bath)
- **Ark Cabins (on-site):** Aug 11–17, **$600**, sleeps up to 4, **only 6
  available** — grab early. Info: https://anarchapulco.com/event/arkcabins2026/
- **Book your own:** Sombor and Apatin have hotels, guesthouses, and
  apartments within reach of the shuttle routes

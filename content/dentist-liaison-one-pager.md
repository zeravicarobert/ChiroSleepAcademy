### The ADA says screen. But then what?

The ADA's policy directs every dental practice to screen patients for
sleep-related breathing disorders. So you screen — and a patient flags
positive. Now what?

They need a diagnosis from a physician. They may eventually need an oral
appliance from you. But there's a step in the middle that almost no one is
providing:

**Someone to evaluate and address the *structure* of the airway —
the cranial, facial, cervical, and postural anatomy that shapes how that
airway behaves.**

That's the step Chiropractic Sleep Academy fills.

---

### What a CSA-certified chiropractor does with your referral

- **Runs a structural airway evaluation** — cranial, facial, cervical,
  and postural assessment of the anatomy behind the obstruction.
- **Coordinates objective testing** (home sleep test) and routes the
  patient to a physician for medical diagnosis when indicated.
- **Diagnoses and addresses the structural airway obstruction** within
  chiropractic scope — the conservative, structure-first phase of care.
- **Refers and co-manages** severe or medically urgent cases with the
  appropriate physician.
- **Sends the patient back to you** — often a better-prepared, more
  successful oral-appliance candidate.

---

### Why this makes your practice stronger

- **A real downstream system** for the patients your screening surfaces —
  not a dead end.
- **A complete, compliant airway pathway:** screen → structure →
  diagnosis → appliance, with each professional in their proper role.
- **Better appliance outcomes.** Patients whose structural contributors
  are addressed first are often better candidates for the therapy you
  provide.
- **You stay the hub.** CSA is your structural partner, not your
  competitor. The patient comes back to you.

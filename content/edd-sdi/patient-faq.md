## Part 1 — The Big Questions

### 1. What is SDI, and how is it different from workers' comp?
SDI stands for **California State Disability Insurance**. It can pay you a portion of your wages when you can't work because of an illness or injury that happened **outside** of work. **Workers' comp only covers injuries that happen on the job — SDI is different.** If your pain, injury, stress, burnout, or sleep problems come from life outside work, SDI is the program that may apply.

### 2. Haven't I already been paying for this?
Yes. If you're a W-2 employee in California, look at your last paycheck for the line marked **"CASDI."** That's money **you** paid into this insurance — a policy you own, funded by California workers' payroll deductions. (Dr. Z often compares it to car insurance: you've been paying premiums; this is your claim.) Most people never use it because they don't know it exists.

### 3. Do I qualify?
You may qualify if **all** of these are true:
- You are (or recently were) a **W-2 worker in California**
- You **worked and earned more than $300** in your base period (roughly the last **5–18 months**)
- You are **unable to do your regular work** because of your condition

Your "base period" is usually **the first 4 of the last 5 completed calendar quarters** before your claim start date. **EDD makes the final decision** on eligibility and amount — Dr. Z's role is honest evaluation and documentation.

### 4. How much money will I receive?
Typically **70–90% of your regular wages** (lower earners are toward the higher end). This is the same rate EDD pays for Paid Family Leave. The **exact amount is set by EDD** from your earnings history — not by our office.

### 5. Can a chiropractor really certify my disability claim?
Yes. Dr. Zeravica is a **licensed California Doctor of Chiropractic with 26 years of experience** helping California workers with recovery and temporary disability. As a licensed provider he can **evaluate and certify** claims for conditions within a chiropractor's scope. He documents your **actual** history, condition, and functional limitations to determine whether certification is medically appropriate.

### 6. Do I have to come into the office?
**No.** Everything is remote: a **free phone consultation**, then a **telehealth evaluation** by phone, then Dr. Z **walks you through submitting your claim** to EDD. No office visits.

### 8. What kinds of conditions can qualify?
Non-work conditions that keep you from your usual work — for example pain or an **injury outside of work**, **stress/burnout**, **sleep problems**, and other functional limitations. Every case is evaluated individually and honestly.

### 9. Is approval guaranteed?
**No — be cautious of anyone who says otherwise.** Submitting a form does **not** guarantee certification or EDD approval. **EDD determines eligibility and benefit amounts.**

### 10. What is this service NOT for?
Not for **Workers' Compensation**, **Federal Disability**, **SSI**, or **SSDI**. It is specifically for **California SDI temporary disability**.

## Part 2 — Filing & Process (the real how-does-this-work questions)

*These are the questions patients actually ask once they're on the phone.*

### 11. Do I need to tell my employer first?
**Yes.** The usual order is: **notify your employer** that you're going on medical/temporary disability leave, **then file your claim with EDD.** Dr. Z will walk you through the sequence.

### 12. Is SDI the same as Paid Family Leave or unemployment?
They're **three different EDD programs:**
- **SDI (Disability):** for **your own** non-work illness or injury — *this is what we help with.*
- **Paid Family Leave (PFL):** to care for a family member or bond with a new child.
- **Unemployment:** when you're **able** to work but out of a job.

SDI and PFL pay at a similar rate (**~70–90%** of income). We focus on **SDI**.

### 13. What date do I use as my disability start date?
Your claim starts when your condition **actually prevents you from doing your usual work.** If you have an ongoing condition that recently **flared up**, the start date reflects that current inability to work — it isn't always the first day you ever felt the symptom. Dr. Z documents the **honest clinical picture**, and EDD determines eligibility from there.

### 14. Do I need all my medical information ready to file?
You can **begin** the claim (the identity and employment portion) before the medical certification is finished. The **medical certification** — the clinical part — is completed by your certifying provider (**Dr. Z**), based on your actual evaluation. He helps you describe your condition **accurately and completely** so EDD has what it needs.

### 15. How do I actually file — is it online?
Yes, most claims are filed through **EDD's SDI Online** portal. **Practical tip from experience:** the EDD site behaves best in **Google Chrome** — patients have hit glitches on **Safari**, so if a page won't load, switch to the Chrome app and log back in. Dr. Z (or the team) can screen-share to help you through it.

### 16. What if my employer sends me forms to fill out?
Send them to **Dr. Z's office** and the team will complete the provider portion and return them. You don't have to figure out the medical paperwork alone.

### 17. Who fills out the medical/provider part of the claim?
**Dr. Z does**, as your certifying provider — documenting your real symptoms and functional limitations. You provide honest information; he provides the clinical certification. **EDD** then decides.

### 18. What will you ask me / what should I have ready?
From the intake: name, DOB, phone, email, **last 4 of SSN**, military service, height/weight; **occupation, employer, last day worked in CA**, whether you're currently working; your **job's physical demands**; **main health problems**, symptom duration, prior treatment/imaging; a **sleep screen** (snoring, witnessed apnea, waking tired, night waking, morning headaches, prior sleep study); and which activities are hard right now.

### 19. Why all the sleep questions?
Because Dr. Z's approach connects **recovery to sleep** — if sleep isn't restored, the body can't fully heal, and healing is what returns you to work. It's part of what makes his evaluation different. *(It is not a promise to cure any condition.)*

### 20. How do I get started (and claim the $50 off)?
Submit your **name, phone, and email** through the intake form or funnel link; the team follows up (usually within 24 hours) to schedule your free consultation and send your coupon code.

## Appendix — Quick Summary: What is "EDD"? (the basics)

*This part just states the obvious.*

- **EDD = Employment Development Department** — the **California state agency** that runs worker programs.
- **It runs three main programs:** **Unemployment**, **State Disability Insurance (SDI)**, and **Paid Family Leave (PFL)**.
- **SDI** is the one that pays part of your wages when you can't work due to a **non-work** illness or injury.
- **In one line:** *EDD is the agency; SDI is the disability benefit it pays out.* Most W-2 employees fund SDI automatically through the **CASDI** deduction on every paycheck.

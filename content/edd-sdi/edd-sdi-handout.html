Part A — The big questions

1.What is SDI, and how is it different from workers' comp?

California State Disability Insurance pays part of your wages when you can't work due to an illness or injury from outside work. Workers' comp only covers on-the-job injuries — SDI is different.

2.Haven't I already been paying for this?

Yes — the "CASDI" line on your paycheck. It's a policy you fund through payroll deductions (Dr. Z compares it to car insurance: you've paid the premiums; this is your claim).

3.Do I qualify?

Usually if all are true: a CA W-2 worker; earned >$300 in your base period (roughly the last 5–18 months); and unable to do your regular work. Base period = first 4 of the last 5 completed calendar quarters. EDD makes the final call.

4.How much will I receive?

Typically 70–90% of your wages (lower earners toward the higher end). EDD sets the exact amount.

5.Can a chiropractor certify my claim?

Yes. Dr. Z is a licensed CA DC (26 years) and certifies claims for conditions within a chiropractor's scope, documenting your real condition and limitations.

6.Do I have to come in?

No — free phone consult, telehealth evaluation by phone, then he walks you through filing. No office visits.

7.What does it cost?

  • Initial phone consultation: Free
  • Starting fee: $400 (evaluation + first month of certification support)
  • Ongoing certification: $150/month, only while medically supported
  • Occasional promo: $50 off first payment
How to pay: Zelle to DrZeravicaOffice@gmail.com (the email, not a phone number; add "Robert Zeravica" as a contact). Office also takes card. ⚠️ Confirm current preferred method before quoting.

8.What conditions can qualify?

Non-work pain or injury, stress/burnout, sleep problems, and other functional limitations that keep you from your usual work. Each case evaluated individually.

9.Is approval guaranteed?

No. Submitting a form doesn't guarantee approval. EDD determines eligibility and amount.

10.What is this NOT for?

Not Workers' Comp, Federal Disability, SSI, or SSDI. Specifically California SDI.

Part B — Filing & process

11.Do I tell my employer first?

Yes — notify your employer of the leave, then file with EDD. Dr. Z walks you through the order.

12.Is SDI the same as Paid Family Leave or unemployment?

Three different EDD programs: SDI = your own non-work illness/injury (what we help with); PFL = caring for family/new child; Unemployment = able to work but jobless.

13.What date do I use as my start date?

When your condition actually prevents your usual work. If an ongoing condition recently flared up, the start date reflects that current inability — documented honestly. EDD decides from there.

14.Do I need all my medical info to file?

You can begin the claim (ID + employment part) before the medical certification is done. The clinical certification is completed by Dr. Z from your actual evaluation.

15.How do I file — online?

Through EDD's SDI Online. Practical tip: the site behaves best in Google Chrome — patients hit glitches on Safari. Team can screen-share to help.

16.What if my employer sends me forms?

Send them to the office; we complete the provider portion and return them.

17.Who fills out the medical part?

Dr. Z, as certifying provider. You give honest info; he provides the clinical certification; EDD decides.

18.What will you ask me / have ready?

Name, DOB, phone, email, last 4 SSN, military service, height/weight; occupation, employer, last day worked in CA, currently working; job's physical demands; main health problems + duration; prior treatment/imaging; sleep screen (snoring, witnessed apnea, waking tired, morning headaches, prior sleep study); which activities are hard now.

19.Why the sleep questions?

Dr. Z's approach connects recovery to sleep — if sleep isn't restored the body can't fully heal. It's what makes his evaluation different. (Not a promise to cure anything.)

20.How do I start (and get the $50 off)?

Submit name, phone, email via the intake form/funnel; team follows up (usually within 24 hrs) to schedule the free consult and send the coupon.

Part C — Payments, status & other forms (real office questions)

21.I paid — what's the status of my certification?

The #1 follow-up. Confirm payment received, give a clear turnaround, and ask for full name + EDD claim number. ⚠️ Confirm the standard turnaround time to quote.

22.EDD gave me a number to give my doctor — what do I do?

Send us that claim number (and any form/receipt number). Dr. Z uses it to find your claim in myEDD/SDI Online and complete the certification.

23.My claim is "awaiting medical certification" — can you complete it?

Yes, that's Dr. Z's role. Provide your claim number and the body part/condition so it matches your evaluation.

24.Can you complete my employer or private-insurance forms?

Often yes — FMLA and carriers like Lincoln Financial, Sedgwick, Voya, Manhattan Life, New York Life, and employer STD packets. Email the forms + deadline. ⚠️ Separate from CA SDI — confirm scope/fee per form.

25.How do I extend my certification?

When you still can't work, we extend it — $150/month while medically supported. Reach out before the current period ends so there's no gap.

26.My claim was denied — can you help me appeal?

The office handles EDD appeals. Send the determination letter + forms and the printed deadline (appeal windows are short). ⚠️ Confirm appeal support offered and any fee.

27.Can I get a refund?

Case-by-case — route to Dr. Z with patient name and reason. ⚠️ No written policy yet — a one-liner would make these consistent.

28.I'm having financial hardship — can you help with the fee?

There's a hardship option in some cases — route to Dr. Z. Handle honestly: hardship is about access, never about certifying a claim that isn't medically supported.

Quick basics — what is "EDD"? The Employment Development Department is the CA state agency running three programs: Unemployment, State Disability Insurance (SDI), and Paid Family Leave. EDD is the agency; SDI is the disability benefit it pays. Most W-2 workers fund SDI via the CASDI paycheck deduction.

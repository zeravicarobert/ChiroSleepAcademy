# Lead-Magnet Strategy — Using the SDI FAQ to Generate Patients

**How the FAQ becomes the top of your funnel.**
Grounded in Dr. Z's existing funnel (lead-gen video → form → free consult → paid eval → monthly certification).

> **Core idea:** The FAQ answers the exact questions that stop a California worker from acting — *"Is this workers' comp? Do I qualify? What's it cost? Do I have to come in? Is it legit?"* Answer those, and the natural next step is a free consult. That's a lead magnet.

---

## 1. Why the FAQ works as a lead magnet

- **It meets skepticism head-on.** People suspect "free money" pitches. A calm, honest FAQ that says *"EDD decides, we don't guarantee approval"* builds the trust that converts.
- **It's the exact top-of-mind content.** These are the most-asked questions — so it's the highest-intent material you can put in front of a lead.
- **It self-qualifies.** By the end, a reader knows if they're a CA W-2 worker who might qualify. Non-fits filter themselves out; fits raise their hand.

## 2. Formats (make the same content work everywhere)

| Format | Where it lives | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **1-page PDF "SDI Benefits FAQ"** | Gated download behind the form | The classic lead magnet — trade the FAQ for name/phone/email |
| **Web page / funnel section** | ClickFunnels site | SEO + always-on; ends in a "Book your free consult" CTA |
| **Short video FAQ** | Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts | One question per clip; each ends with the funnel link |
| **Printed flyer / QR** | Partner offices, community spots | Drives to the same form |
| **Text/DM reply asset** | ChiroCat / DMs | When someone asks "how does this work?", send the FAQ |

**One source, many surfaces** — the FAQ file is written so it drops into all of these.

## 3. The funnel it feeds (your existing flow, sharpened)

```
FAQ (video / page / PDF / flyer)
        │  hook: "You may already own a disability policy — here's how it works"
        ▼
Lead form  →  name, phone, email  (+ optional $50-off coupon)
        ▼
Free phone consultation   (screen for fit)
        ▼
$400 telehealth evaluation  (+ first month certification)
        ▼
$150 / month certification   ← recurring revenue
        ▼
Recovery track → Sleep/airway → CSA
```

The FAQ's only job is to move a stranger to the **free consult**. Every format ends with that one CTA.

## 4. The hook + the CTA (keep them consistent)

- **Hook (from your own script):** *"Are you a California W-2 worker? You may already own a disability insurance policy — and not know it."*
- **Trust line:** *"26 years helping California workers. Honest evaluation — EDD makes the final decision."*
- **CTA:** *"Drop your name, phone, and email to book a free consultation and get your $50-off code."*

**Recommendation:** Use the identical hook/trust/CTA across every format so the message compounds instead of fragmenting.

## 5. Distribution (where to put it)

1. **Organic social** — one FAQ question per short video; cheap, repeatable, your voice.
2. **Existing database** — the ~28 opt-in DCs and your patient list already know you; the FAQ is a soft re-engagement.
3. **Partner placement** — flyers/QR where CA W-2 workers are (allied clinics, community boards) — within advertising rules.
4. **Warm referrals** — current SDI patients + the "referral source" field on your intake make word-of-mouth trackable.
5. **Paid ads later** — only after organic proves the message and you've confirmed the compliance framing holds up.

## 6. It's a lead magnet for TWO businesses

Every SDI lead is also a **sleep/airway lead.** The FAQ's sleep questions (snoring, waking tired, morning headaches) prime the recovery conversation. So the same FAQ that fills the SDI pipeline also feeds:

- **CSA** (Structural Airway Chiropractic) — the DC-facing movement
- The **patient recovery track** (HST → CBCT → structural care)

One lead magnet, two revenue systems.

## 7. Measure what matters

Track the funnel, not vanity metrics:
- FAQ views → **form submissions** (capture rate)
- Form → **consult booked** (show rate)
- Consult → **paid evaluation** (close rate)
- Eval → **months retained** (LTV)
- Source field on intake → **which channel actually produces paying patients**

**Recommendation:** Put these five numbers on one simple sheet. They tell you where the funnel leaks and where to spend next.

---

## Compliance notes for the lead magnet

- Keep the FAQ's honesty intact in every format — *no guarantees, EDD decides, honest documentation.* The trust is the conversion mechanism; don't trade it for a punchier promise.
- Follow advertising rules for healthcare and disability services; avoid anything that reads as inducing false claims.
- The $50-off is a **timing** incentive to book a consult — never framed as a discount on "getting approved."

*The most persuasive thing you can put in front of a skeptical worker is the truth, clearly organized. That's what makes the FAQ your best lead magnet.*

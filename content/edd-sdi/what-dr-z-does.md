## 2. The problem you solve

Most California W-2 workers don't know three things:

1. They **already own** a disability insurance policy — the "CASDI" deduction on every paycheck.
2. SDI is **not** workers' comp. It covers illness, injury, pain, stress, burnout, and sleep problems that come from **life outside of work**.
3. There's a **licensed provider** who can evaluate them and certify a claim **without a single office visit.**

The result: a benefit workers funded for years goes unused because it feels confusing, bureaucratic, or "not for them." You remove that friction.

## 3. Who it serves

- **California W-2 employees** who worked and earned **more than $300** in their base period (roughly the last **5–18 months**) and are **unable to do their regular work**.
- People dealing with **non-work** pain, injury, stress/burnout, or **sleep dysfunction** and other functional limitations.
- **Not** for: Workers' Comp, Federal Disability, SSI, or SSDI. (Stated plainly on the screening page.)

## 4. The belief that makes it more than paperwork

From your **Temporary Disability-to-Reinvention Pathway**:

> *"Most people who go on temporary disability are suffering from hidden sleep and airway problems that prevent the body from healing and returning to work."*

That belief is what separates you from a claims mill. You're not just certifying time off — you're using the disability window to **restore capacity**, starting with sleep. For patients: *"If your sleep isn't restored, your body cannot fully heal."* This is the bridge from the EDD service into CSA / Structural Airway Chiropractic.

## 5. The patient journey (what actually happens)

| Step | What happens | Who does it | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Lead** | Sees a video/flyer/post, submits name + phone + email | Patient → intake form / funnel | Free |
| **1. Free consult** | Short phone call to talk through the situation and screen for fit | Dr. Z / team | Free |
| **2. Telehealth evaluation** | Phone medical evaluation; history, condition, functional limits, sleep screen | Dr. Z | **$400** (incl. first month of certification support) |
| **3. Claim submission** | Patient signs a **Power of Attorney** so Dr. Z can complete the CA EDD claim form (the "red forms"); form submitted to EDD | Dr. Z, with patient | Included |
| **4. EDD decision** | EDD determines eligibility and benefit amount | **EDD (state)** | — |
| **5. Ongoing certification** | Monthly re-certification for as long as disability remains medically supported; work letters, SOAP/treatment notes as needed | Dr. Z / team | **$150 / month** |
| **6. Recovery + cross-sell** | Sleep/airway evaluation, structural care, referral into CSA pathway | Dr. Z / CSA | à la carte |

Everything through Step 5 is **remote** — no office visit.

## 6. Your role vs. everyone else's role

- **Dr. Z (DC):** Screens, evaluates, documents, certifies within a chiropractor's scope, completes and submits the EDD forms, and provides monthly certification support. Explains findings through the structural/sleep lens.
- **EDD (the state):** The final authority. Determines **eligibility and benefit amount**. Your service never guarantees approval.
- **The patient:** Answers honestly, signs the POA, and follows the care plan.
- **Support team:** Peter (operations), front desk (e.g., Delfina) for scheduling, intake, and records; Santiago and others referenced in ops threads.

## 7. The honest-documentation guardrail (non-negotiable)

Your own screening page says it best:

> *"This is not about gaming the system. This is about proper evaluation, honest documentation, and determining whether your condition may support temporary disability certification under CA SDI."*

Every part of the service — marketing, intake, evaluation, monetization — has to stay on this side of the line. It protects your license, your patients, and the credibility of the whole operation. **This is the same credibility discipline as CSA: honest about what's supported, never overselling.**

- Sleep screen: loud snoring, witnessed apnea, waking tired, night waking, morning headaches, prior sleep study

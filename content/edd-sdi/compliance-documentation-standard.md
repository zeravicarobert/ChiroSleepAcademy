# EDD / SDI — Documentation & Compliance Standard (Team)

**Purpose:** keep every certification honest, in-scope, and defensible. This protects Dr. Z's license, the patients, and the business.
**Status:** internal draft — **have a California healthcare/licensing attorney review and finalize before relying on it.** (Not legal advice.)

---

## The one rule everything follows
> We **document what is medically true** and let **EDD (or the insurer) decide.** We never promise approval, never shape a story to get a claim approved, and never certify a disability that isn't genuinely supported by an evaluation.

## 1. Scope — what a California DC may certify
- ✅ **Physical / neuromusculoskeletal conditions within CA chiropractic scope** — spine and **all joints/extremities** (e.g., back, neck, **knee, shoulder**), muscles, and connective tissue.
- ❌ **Mental-health / psychiatric conditions** — stress, anxiety, depression, "burnout" as the disabling diagnosis. EDD accepts a chiropractor's certification **only for physical conditions.** Refer these to an appropriate provider (MD, psychiatrist, psychologist, LCSW) to certify.
- ❌ Anything requiring the **practice of medicine** (prescribing, etc.).
- **If a claim's real driver is mental health,** don't certify it as a physical claim to fit our scope — refer it out.

## 2. Every certification requires a real, individual evaluation
- No certification without a documented telehealth (or in-person) evaluation of **that** patient for **that** condition.
- The evaluation and findings live in the chart **before** the cert is submitted.
- Volume never substitutes for an individual clinical judgment.

## 3. Diagnoses come from the clinician — never the patient
- **Patients do not self-select diagnoses or ICD-10 codes.** Retire/replace any form that has patients check which conditions they "have."
- The clinician assigns diagnoses based on history + exam findings.

## 4. Claim start date
- The start date reflects **when the condition actually prevents the patient's usual work** (a genuine current flare-up counts).
- No back-dating to inflate benefits or fit a desired payout.

## 5. Private-insurer & FMLA forms (Lincoln, Sedgwick, Voya, Manhattan Life, NY Life, employer STD)
- **Same standard as EDD.** A false or exaggerated certification to a private insurer is insurance fraud.
- Only certify within scope and only what the evaluation supports.

## 6. Fees are for the service, never for the outcome
- Fees ($400 start, $150/mo) pay for evaluation, coordination, and certification **work** — never contingent on approval or benefit amount.
- Hardship accommodations are about **access to the service**, never about certifying an unsupported claim.

## 7. Records to keep for every patient
- Signed intake, evaluation notes, exam findings, diagnosis rationale, the certification submitted, and any insurer forms.
- Enough that an outside reviewer could see **why** each certification was medically appropriate.

## Red lines (never do)
- Certify a disability not supported by an evaluation.
- Certify outside chiropractic scope (esp. mental-health conditions).
- Let patients pick their own diagnoses/codes.
- Back-date or misstate facts to EDD or an insurer.
- Market or word anything as coaching a claim toward approval.

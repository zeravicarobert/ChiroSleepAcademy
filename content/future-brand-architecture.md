## ⚠️ Robert's own flag on "Lifestyle" (hold this)
Robert's concern: **"lifestyle," colloquially, can carry a sexuality connotation** ("the
lifestyle") — he does **not** want that association. His intended meaning is **healthier
living / stewardship** (NEWSTART-style: nutrition, movement, rest, breath, etc.).

Honest brand-guardian notes for when he revisits:
- **The connotation is dated and context-dependent.** In health, **"Lifestyle
  Medicine"** is an established, respectable field (American College of Lifestyle
  Medicine). In a health/stewardship frame, "Lifestyle Academy" reads **wellness**, not
  sexuality. So the risk is lower than it feels — but it's a real judgment call.
- **If he wants zero ambiguity, umbrella alternatives to weigh later:** *The Stewardship
  Academy · The Whole-Life Academy · The Living Well Academy · The Vital Living Academy ·
  The NEWSTART Academy · The Wholeness Academy · The Restoration Academy.* ("Restoration"
  is already used one level down, so avoid double-use.)

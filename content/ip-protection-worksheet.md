# IP Protection Worksheet — Trademarks & Copyrights

**Purpose:** a hand-off list for Robert's attorney. Claude can *organize and
list*; Claude **cannot file** — trademarks go through the USPTO (or state),
copyrights through the U.S. Copyright Office.
**⚠️ Not legal advice.** An IP attorney confirms what's protectable and files.
Ties to Master Summary Priority 1 (legal) and `funnel/patient-study-launch-checklist.md`.

**Quick primer (plain):**
- **Trademark = protects NAMES / brands** used in business (e.g., VAET, CSA).
  Use **™ now** (unregistered), **®** only after registration. USPTO filing fee
  ~$250–350 per class + attorney; **confirm current fees.**
- **Copyright = protects WRITTEN WORKS** (books, curriculum). It's **automatic
  the moment you write it** — you already own it. **Registration** (~$45–65 per
  work; confirm) gives you the standing to enforce/sue. Use **© 2026 Robert
  Zeravica** now.

# Analogy — "The Original Protocol" (Bitcoin whitepaper → Chiropractic)

**Robert-authored framing, 2026-07-16.** A reusable persuasion analogy for people
who think in **"the original was hijacked; the real thing must be restored"** —
crypto folks (esp. BSV / "real Bitcoin"), sovereignty thinkers, first-principles
people. Born in a thread with Eleanor (BlockchainAcademy teacher who sees BSV as
the true Bitcoin).

## The parallel
| Bitcoin (their belief) | Chiropractic (CSA's story) |
|---|---|
| An **original design** — the 8-page whitepaper | An **original principle** — "structure governs function," ADIO, the whole body |
| Got **hijacked / narrowed** away from Satoshi's vision | Got **boxed into "back pain"** — pain management, a tiny slice |
| **BSV** = the real thing restored | **CSA** = chiropractic restored to its original, whole-body design |
| Restore the original protocol | Restore the original protocol — sleep, airway, structure, whole-body healing |

## The line
> You say Bitcoin got hijacked from its original design and the real thing has to
> be restored. Chiropractic has the same story — it got boxed into back pain, when
> "structure governs function" was always about the whole body. CSA restores the
> original protocol: sleep, airway, structure. **Same fight. Restore the original.**

## Why it lands
- Speaks the listener's **existing worldview** — they feel understood, not pitched.
- The parallel is **real, not a stretch:** "structure governs function" genuinely is
  chiropractic's founding principle; pain-only really is a narrowing of it.
- Ties to the **pain → sleep** repositioning and the **decision-maker** audience.

## ⚠️ Guardrails
- **"Hijacked" is rapport language for the right listener — not public CSA copy.**
  Don't put conspiracy framing in the website, workbook, or MD/DDS materials
  (red lines §5: no fringe/anti-science). Keep it for one-to-one conversations with
  people who already think this way.
- CSA's actual claims stay **honest**: repositioning + measured outcomes, **no cure
  claims**, respectful of medicine.
- Use the analogy to explain the *mission*, then let the real evidence (outcomes)
  carry the *proof*.

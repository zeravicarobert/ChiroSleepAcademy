# CSA Gems — Pull-Quote Bank

Lines for slides, social, email, and talks. Every line here is checked against
the red lines in `CLAUDE.md` section 5.

**Rule for this file:** a line only earns a place if it is *true as stated
standing alone*, with no surrounding paragraph to rescue it. A gem gets quoted
without its context. That is what a gem is for, and that is what makes a sloppy
one dangerous.

---

## Tier 1 — The category lines

These carry the most weight. Lead with these.

> **"Physiology lives inside anatomy."**

The best line CSA has. It concedes the entire multifactorial argument and still
leaves the structural question standing. It does not fight sleep medicine — it
accepts everything sleep medicine says and asks about the container. Additive,
not competitive. Use it everywhere.

> **"Same fluid. Different structure. Different geometry. Different mechanical outcome."**

Four beats, whole argument. This is the slide.

> **"Alignment gives anatomy room to express its better geometry."**

> **"Sleep airway structure is the physical framework within which multifactorial
> physiology operates."**

The long, formal version of gem one. Use in written material where precision
matters more than punch.

---

## Tier 2 — Mechanism lines

> **"The airway is not a dry pipe. It is a wet, living, collapsible structure
> lined with fluid."**

> **"Realignment may reduce the mechanical consequences of surface tension by
> increasing separation between wet airway tissues."**

Long, but every word is load-bearing. Do not shorten it. See the retired section.

> **"Structural alignment determines, in part, how close wet tissues get — and
> therefore how much opportunity surface tension has to matter."**

> **"They changed the fluid. Nobody changed the geometry."**

The research-positioning line. Strong with a clinical or academic audience
because it names a real gap rather than a belief.

> **"Structure sets the threshold at which the fluid force gets to participate
> at all."**

---

## Tier 3 — Framing and posture

> **"We are not competing with CPAP, oral appliances, or sleep medicine. We are
> adding the piece the airway conversation has been missing."**

> **"Chiropractors do not diagnose sleep apnea. The role is structural."**

Say this early and often. It buys permission for everything else.

> **"Anatomy establishes the space. Geometry determines proximity. Proximity
> determines whether wet tissues meet."**

> **"The home sleep test is the sleep-and-airway equivalent of the X-ray."**

Internal / DC-facing. It reframes the clinical workflow instantly for a
chiropractor.

---

## RETIRED — do not use

> ~~"Realignment reduces surface tension."~~

**Why it is retired:** it is false. Surface tension is a property of the fluid.
Structural change does not alter saliva chemistry and does not lower surface
tension. What structural change may alter is *how much that surface tension
matters mechanically*, by increasing the distance between wet surfaces.

This is checkable in thirty seconds by anyone with a physiology background. It
is the single fastest way to lose a dentist, an ENT, or a sleep physician — and
it violates the "never exaggerate the mechanism" red line.

The correct version is in Tier 2. It is longer. Use the longer one.

**The principle this illustrates:** the precision *is* the credibility. Every
time a line gets shortened for punch, check whether the shortening made it
false. In this field, the careful version outperforms the punchy version with
every audience that matters.

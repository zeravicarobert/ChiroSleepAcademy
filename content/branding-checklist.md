## Pronunciation + spelling cues (for both Robert and post-production text)

- **Domain:** ChiropracticSleepAcademy.net — say **"dot net"** clearly.
  NOT .com, NOT .org. **DOT NET.**
- **Name spell-out (for Block 8 + lower-thirds):** Z-E-R-A-V-I-C-A
- **Credential:** **DC** (Doctor of Chiropractic) — never "Dr. Robert"
  alone in the close; always "Dr. Robert Zeravica, DC"
- **Brand name:** Chiropractic Sleep Academy — never abbreviate as
  "CSA" on camera; spell it out so first-time viewers hear the full
  category name. *"CSA" is internal shorthand only.*

---

## Why this discipline matters

Without it, the videos are good teaching that go nowhere. With it,
every clip becomes a referral path. Three things compound:

1. **Recall** — viewers hear the same name + domain across every
   clip, dozens of times. The brand sticks.
2. **Routing** — every clip ends with a destination. A viewer who
   wants more knows exactly where to go.
3. **SEO + share value** — when clips get shared, the verbal mention
   of the domain survives even if the lower-third gets cropped on
   social.

## Faith register — the credo (movement / book / DC-faith venues)

For one hundred years, Doctors of Chiropractic looked at the spine.
For fifty years, dentists looked at the jaw.
For fifty years, medicine looked at apnea.

But no one looked at the entire body —
from the skull to the feet —
as ONE biomechanical temple designed to breathe.

This is the birth of a new profession.
A new worldview.
A new mandate.

Sleep Chiropractic exists because the world has misunderstood sleep for too long.

Sleep isn't only about the lungs.
Or the throat.
Or the nose.
It is shaped from the skull down —
through the dura,
through the rhythms that God placed in the human frame.

When you free the body's rhythms,
breath can open,
sleep can deepen,
and life can flourish.

This is not technique.
This is stewardship.
This is worship.
This is chiropractic fulfilling its divine purpose.

Welcome to The Skull-Down Review.

## Secular twin (MD / DDS / patient / professional venues)

For a century, Doctors of Chiropractic studied the spine.
For decades, dentists studied the jaw.
For decades, medicine studied the apnea event itself.

Each saw a real part. None saw the whole.

**The body breathes as one integrated system — from the skull to the feet.** Sleep and
airway aren't only a throat problem or a jaw problem. They're shaped by the whole
structural frame the airway sits in.

That whole-body structure is an **under-addressed dimension** of sleep and airway care.
CPAP and oral appliances hold the airway open — and for many patients they're the right
tool. But they work only when they're used, and a large share of patients can't or won't
tolerate them. What none of these tools address is the **structure around the airway.**

That is the gap the Doctor of Chiropractic is equipped to **assess and manage** — measured
with testing, and coordinated with the dentist and physician **as peers.**

This is the **Skull-Down model:** assess the whole chain — cranial mechanics, cervical
curve, thoracic and diaphragmatic movement, pelvis, and even foot posture and gait —
because each **may influence** how the airway performs at night.

**A body breathes. Our job is to make sure the structure lets it.**

## Editor's notes (fixes applied)
Three overclaim fixes to the faith credo, per the review:
1. **Outcome promise softened.** "breath returns. Sleep returns. Life returns." →
   "breath **can** open, sleep **can** deepen, life **can** flourish." (No guaranteed
   cure — §5.)
2. **Retired verb swapped.** "**restore** the mechanical rhythms" → "**free** the
   mechanical rhythms." (Canon verbs: Unsubluxate/Expand/Establish/Stabilize/Free.)
3. **Mechanism-as-fact → worldview, additive not dismissive.** "Sleep does not begin in
   the lungs/throat/nose. It begins in the skull." → "Sleep **isn't only** about the
   lungs/throat/nose. It is **shaped from the skull down**." (Keeps skull-down primacy
   without denying airway science.)

Also: "chiropractors" → "Doctors of Chiropractic" (copy rule); "apnea, 30 years" → "fifty
years" (accuracy — OSA described mid-1960s–70s).

**Secular twin** strips faith (worship/ministry/divine/God) and the CPAP/appliance attack;
keeps the skull-down whole-body thesis, "under-addressed dimension," measured +
peer-collaborative framing, and "a body breathes" as the shared through-line. Use the
**credo** for believers/DCs/the movement; use the **secular twin** anywhere an MD, DDS, or
patient will read it.

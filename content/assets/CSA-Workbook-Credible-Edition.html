Part I

Foundations of Structural Airway Chiropractic

Ch 1 — History & Philosophy

Chiropractic began in the late 19th century on the principle that structure governs function. D.D. Palmer founded it alongside an era rich in anatomy, neurology, and vitalism; A.T. Still founded osteopathy in the same period. CSA is chiropractic — it studies the whole body and learns freely from any tradition, but is not "osteopathic." California matured chiropractic into a licensed, diagnosing profession within its defined scope.

Ch 2 — From Dogma to Discernment governing method

CSA's operating rule, plainly: we do not judge truth claims; we judge clinical consequence. A technique earns standing by what it measurably produces, not by the confidence of its story. Hold strong roots, test everything, keep what works, and report honestly — including what doesn't respond.

Ch 3 — Innate Intelligence & Primary Respiratory Mechanism

Innate intelligence — the body's organizing, self-healing capacity — is expressed in measurable ways (cellular signaling, tissue adaptation, neural plasticity); taught as biology, not mysticism. PRI/PRM and the Reciprocal Tension Membrane / dura are presented as cranial-osteopathic models under investigation — useful lenses, not settled physiology.

Ch 4 — Anatomy for Airway Function established

  • Viscerocranium & sphenoid — facial skeleton and keystone bone shaping airway aperture and sinus drainage.
  • Hyoid apparatus — suspends tongue and larynx; governs tongue posture and airway patency.
  • Mandibular plane — a measurable indicator of craniofacial growth and airway volume.

Ch 5 — Tensegrity / Biotensegrity core framing

Tensegrity (Fuller; Snelson) and its biological application biotensegrity (Stephen Levin, MD) describe the body as a pre-stressed, load-sharing network: bones float within a continuous tension web. CSA applies this established framing to the cranium and airway — we did not invent it. The teaching phrase: motion is distributed, deformation is shared; no single joint "moves" — the system yields. Suture micromotion and compliance under load are real; the degree and its sleep/airway significance are what CSA is testing. The credible replacement for "cranial pump" language.

Part II

Sleep Science & Airway Mechanics

Ch 6 — Upper-Airway Obstructive Sleep Apnea (UAOSA)

Mechanical collapse of the upper airway during sleep → intermittent desaturation, micro-arousals, systemic stress. CSA's contribution is a biomechanical lens: craniofacial restriction, TMJ/hyoid imbalance, forward-head posture / cervical hypolordosis, and pelvic compensation as possible contributors — in addition to, not instead of, the recognized medical picture.

Ch 7 — Sleep Homeostasis mainstream

Adenosine accumulates during wakefulness and builds sleep pressure. (A proposed hypothesis that adenosine concentrates in cranial/sphenoid structures is unproven — present it as a question to investigate, never as fact.)

Ch 8 — Testing & Severity HST + clinical read

The Home Sleep Test (HST) records O₂ saturation, airflow, snoring, and AHI — ordered through a licensed HST pathway; the DC reads the case clinically and diagnoses it. AHI (AASM): Normal <5 · Mild 5–14 · Moderate 15–29 · Severe ≥30. The DC correlates numbers with structural findings within scope.

Ch 9 — The Consequence Standard in Practice

Document before and after: posture photos, CBCT/lateral ceph airway volume, HST metrics (AHI/ODI/SpO₂), patient-reported outcomes. Report responders and non-responders. Improvement shows structure contributed in that case — never a cure, never proof of mechanism. Data → the CSA Outcomes Registry.

Ch 10 — Report of Findings, Education & Scope

Core scope rule: sleep and airway are biomechanical problems that cross licensure lines; CSA is where those lines collaborate without collapsing scope. Screen, coordinate testing, educate, co-manage, refer.

Part III

VAET™ as a Framework

Ch 11 — What VAET Is

VAET is not a new cranial technique but a condition-specific application framework sequencing established methods (CFR/BNS, SOT, AK, CBP, postural-foundation work) for the airway indication, in a defined order and time-limited window. Offered as a legitimate early structural intervention, honestly framed — always measured (pre/post HST), co-managed, and never positioned as superior to or a replacement for CPAP, appliances, or surgery.

Ch 12 — CFR / BNS with safety

CFR mobilizes cranial sutures via the nasal passages (Dr. Z certified since 2012), within a full-body structural model; BNS is a related nasal/cranial method. Safety: sterile nasal balloons only; contraindications include recent nasal surgery, severe sinus infection, uncontrolled hypertension; obtain informed consent; document nasal patency pre/post.

Ch 13 — SOT, CBP & Postural Foundation

SOT pelvic categories I–III and CBP cervical-lordosis correction are integrated and measured (e.g., pre/post cervical angles). Any claim that lordosis change reduces AHI is a hypothesis to test per case, reported honestly — not a promised result.

Ch 14 — Cerebrospinal Fluid

Teach precisely: CSF exchange is rhythmic and sleep-enhanced (pulsatile, glymphatic — established). Whether cranial mechanics assist it is CSA's model under investigation. State the two clauses separately. ("Liquid Light"/"trampoline effect" are internal metaphors only — not professional physiology.)

Ch 15 — Documentation

SOAP notes; VAET session findings; pre/post metrics; re-evaluation timeline. Documentation converts belief into evidence.

Part IV

Whole-Person Support

Ch 16 — Lifestyle Foundations evidence-based only

Sleep, nutrition, movement, weight where relevant, hydration, and stress each influence airway and recovery. Teach the evidence-based basics (e.g., NEW START / lifestyle-medicine principles as habit prescriptions), measured like everything else.

What was removed, and why. The prior draft's energy/frequency "medicine" (scalar-energy devices, Rife machines, "electromagnetic vitality," ozone otic/rectal insufflation, alkaline/deuterium-depleted water, colloidal products) and its commercial product endorsements are removed from the professional CE curriculum. They read as pseudoscience to the MD/DDS/college audience CSA needs, some carry safety/regulatory concerns, and endorsements create conflict-of-interest exposure. Any personal product use belongs in a separate, clearly-labeled resource with disclosure — never inside the board-approved clinical curriculum.
Part V

Applied Clinical Protocols

Ch 17 — Screening & Patient Education

Mallampati class as an education/communication tool (not a diagnostic test); snoring/witnessed-apnea logs; STOP-BANG/Epworth screening; the structural airway exam (Mallampati, tongue scalloping, forward-head, cranial- postural symmetry, asymmetric palatal yield as a documented observation to correlate — not a diagnosis).

Ch 18 — Case Documentation & the Registry

De-identified case records (Case IDs, no PHI in shared trackers); outcomes reported in full including non-responders; feeding the CSA Outcomes Registry — the honest evidence base that earns the profession's respect.

Ch 19 — Scope & Referral Logic

When to screen, test, co-manage, refer (MD/ENT/DDS). Every provider stays in their lane; CSA coordinates. Moderate–severe → medical co-management.

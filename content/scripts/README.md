# CSA Patient-Flow Scripts — Consult · Structural Airway Exam · Report of Findings

These are the CSA adaptations of the classic **AMC** Consultation / Exam /
Report scripts (Tom Owen III and Dr. Bearden, transcribed by Dr. Z). They
keep AMC's proven *relational craft* and drop everything that CSA's red
lines forbid.

## The pathway (from CLAUDE.md §7, Phase 2)

Old DC flow: **Consult → Exam → X-ray → Report of Findings**
CSA flow: **Consult → Structural Airway Exam → Home Sleep Test (HST) →
Report of Findings**

> The HST is the sleep-and-airway equivalent of the X-ray. It is the
> objective data that anchors the clinical conversation. (CBCT is the
> Step-3 Airway Baseline; see CLAUDE.md §8.)

## What we KEEP from AMC (the craft)

- **Door-knob professionalism** — the 3–5 minute wait, the busy, prepared
  doctor, knowing exactly what happens on the other side of the door.
- **Consult = Ear · Exam = Eye · Report = Mouth.** In the consult you
  listen; speak half as much as you think you should.
- **The three crucial parts of a consult:** Intro, Identify the Problem,
  Close.
- **Honest-exclamatory findings** — the patient never takes the condition
  more seriously than the doctor does. (We are exclamatory only about
  findings that are *real*. Never manufactured.)
- **Bring the bed partner in.** In sleep this is not a sales tactic — the
  bed partner is a genuine clinical witness (they hear the snoring, see
  the pauses) and the at-home support for follow-through.
- **Explain every instrument** before using it.
- **Anchor on objective data**, then return for a proper Report of
  Findings. The Report is where the case is understood, not oversold.

## What we STRIP (AMC psychology CSA forbids — CLAUDE.md §5)

- **No fear-selling.** No "this will give you a heart attack." We educate
  soberly and honestly about *associated* risks, and route to proper
  diagnosis — not to a scare.
- **No cure claims.** Chiropractic/VAET does not cure sleep apnea. The DC
  role is **structural**. The DC does not diagnose OSA — a physician
  reads the HST and diagnoses.
- **No manufactured urgency or "need behind the need" as a closing lever.**
  The patient makes an informed decision; we protect their interest over
  the sale (ethical financial close).
- **No over-spiritualizing.** Faith lives in the Founder's Creed, not in
  patient-facing clinical copy. These scripts stay clinically clean.

## The clinical translation (AMC → CSA)

| AMC | CSA |
|---|---|
| Subluxation | Structural + airway contribution to disordered breathing/sleep |
| 5 factors of a subluxation | The Airway & Structure screen (5 indicators) |
| Nervoscope / instruments | SnoreLab, pulse-ox ring, HST device, Epworth/STOP-BANG, Mallampati |
| X-ray / "films" | Home Sleep Test (HST); CBCT at Airway Baseline |
| Report of Findings on films | Report of Findings on the HST |
| "Test adjustment" | Honest recommended pathway + freedom to use other tables |

## The posture: potluck / first table (CLAUDE.md §1)

CSA's table is the **first table** — early structural intervention. The
Report names, plainly, that the patient is free to go to the other tables
(sleep physician, dentist, ENT, surgeon) for what they genuinely need,
and free to pass a dish by. Early intervention first; everything else by
real need, never by fear.

## Note on the DC-as-patient use (Heal the Healer First)

These are patient-facing, but they work verbatim when the "patient" is a
DC going through CSA's Phase 1. Dr. Z runs the same flow on the doctor
first; the doctor then learns the system by having lived it.

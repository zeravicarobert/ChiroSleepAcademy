How to read this — and how to keep it honest.
  • De-identified only. No names, DOB, or record numbers on any slide. PHI stays in the EHR.
  • Report every case, including non-responders. A case that didn't improve is data, not a failure to hide.
  • Measurement, not a cure claim. Improvement shows structure contributed in this case — never that apnea is cured or that VAET beats CPAP, appliances, or surgery.
  • DC-read and managed. In California the Doctor of Chiropractic reads the case clinically, diagnoses, and manages it within full scope, and co-manages complex cases with an MD/ENT as peers, by choice.
  • AHI severity bands (AASM): Normal <5 · Mild 5–15 · Moderate 15–30 · Severe 30+ events/hr.

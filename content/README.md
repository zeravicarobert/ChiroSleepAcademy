## ⚠️ Two factual items to verify BEFORE recording

Robert flagged these as "true?" — both need correction before they
go on camera (credibility per §5):

1. **CSF production location.** CSF is primarily produced by the
   **choroid plexus in all four ventricles, but predominantly the
   LATERAL ventricles** (not the 4th alone). The 4th ventricle is
   the **exit point** — CSF flows out through the foramina of
   Magendie and Luschka into the subarachnoid space. Record:
   *"CSF is made by the choroid plexus, mostly in the lateral
   ventricles, and exits via the 4th ventricle."*

2. **Pituitary secretion into CSF.** The **anterior pituitary**
   secretes its hormones (ACTH, TSH, FSH, LH, GH, Prolactin) into
   the **hypophyseal portal blood system**, NOT directly into CSF.
   The **hypothalamus** is the structure with documented secretion
   into CSF (3rd ventricle). The sella turcica is the bony seat of
   the pituitary but is not a CSF release point in the way Robert
   described. **Suggest reframing** the on-camera teaching around
   hypothalamic-CSF communication, OR have Robert verify the
   specific anterior-pituitary-into-CSF claim against a current
   neuroanatomy text before recording.

If either correction changes Robert's planned framing, log the
adjusted version in `chiro-2.0-triad-of-health.md` before recording.

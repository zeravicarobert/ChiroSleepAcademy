## Usage rules (readability + print sanity)
- **Covers, branding, headers, accents, short premium leave-behinds** → full
  **black + matte gold** (dark, luxury). e.g., the patient-to-dentist handoff, section
  dividers, one-pagers.
- **Long-form / fillable workbooks** (pages of reading, checklists a clinician writes
  on) → **light interior** (cream/white page, black body text, **matte-gold headings
  and accents**). A fully black 11-page workbook is hard to read and ink-heavy; keep
  the gold for the frame, not the whole page.
- **Matte gold, not yellow.** Keep it desaturated and warm; never a bright/neon gold.
- **One brand across all audiences.** Don't color-code by audience (that was the old
  navy/teal mistake). DC vs. DDS vs. patient is distinguished by **title and content**,
  not by different colors. Everything is black + matte gold.

## Confirmed interior rule (Robert, 2026-07-16)
**Inside** any book/booklet/workbook: **white pages, black ink text, matte-gold
accents.** (Covers and short premium leave-behinds may be full black + gold.) On
white, keep gold for **rules, eyebrows, dividers, bullets, and black-ground table
headers** — headings stay black/dark for readability (bright gold text on white is
low-contrast). Use a darker "antique gold" for any small gold text on white.

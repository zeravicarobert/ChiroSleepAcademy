## Executive Summary

Sleep apnea is increasingly treated with oral appliances. Access has grown, but
outcomes stay inconsistent.

The reason is not the device. It is the **missing role** around the device.

There is a layer of sleep-apnea care that no discipline currently owns:
**biomechanical systems integration** — how a change in the jaw moves through the
spine, pelvis, cranium, and load transfer, over time.

That layer is not dental. It is not device engineering. **It is chiropractic.**
This paper makes the case that the profession trained to see the body as one
coordinated system is the profession built to fill this gap — and that
Chiropractic Sleep Academy is the place to learn how.

---

## The Training Silos — and the Chiropractic Advantage

**Dentistry** is trained (largely by manufacturers) on airway patency,
occlusion, and appliance delivery. Excellent within its scope — but with little
exposure to whole-body stomatognathic biomechanics.

**Manufacturers** are experts in device design. They train on fit, compliance,
and mechanics — not on spinal compensation, postural load shifts, or
cranial–cervical consequences.

**Chiropractic** is the one profession already trained, through post-graduate
work, to read the whole kinetic chain:

- **CBP** — spinal sagittal-curve correction
- **SOT / AK** — pelvic–cranial category organization
- **CFR / BNS** — viscerocranial–neurocranial mechanics
- **Foot Levelers** — ground-up load transfer through the foot arches

The tragedy is that this knowledge is **rarely invited into the appliance
workflow.** The DC is the missing seat at the table.

## The Missing Layer: Biomechanical Systems Integration

Sleep apnea is not a jaw-only disorder, not a dental-only disorder, and not a
single-modality condition.

It is a **whole-body biomechanical disorder with airway expression.**

The missing question — the one nobody currently owns — is:

> *When the jaw changes, what happens to the spine, pelvis, cranium, and load
> transfer — over time?*

Chiropractic is the profession that thinks in exactly this way.

## The Chiropractic-Philosophy Case: Systems Intelligence

Here is where chiropractic offers something no other profession can.

Chiropractic has always rested on two ideas:

- **Universal Intelligence** — the order expressed in all matter.
- **Innate Intelligence** — the body's inborn wisdom, always working toward
  health.

Sleep-apnea care exposes a gap *between* these two. Innate Intelligence is doing
its job — the body compensates for a compromised airway all night long. But the
**coordination** between structures — jaw, cranium, cervical spine, pelvis, feet
— is where care breaks down. Each provider optimizes one part; no one tends the
whole system.

CSA names this missing link **Systems Intelligence:** the layer that recognizes
the body as one coordinated structural system and organizes care accordingly.
It is the bridge between the universal order chiropractic has always taught and
the innate healing chiropractic has always served — applied to the airway.

> *Universal Intelligence sets the order. Innate Intelligence runs the body.
> Systems Intelligence is how we honor both at once — by treating sleep apnea as
> one system, not many parts.*

## The Corrected Model (with DC services included)

**Phase 1 — Entry & Screening (DDS).** Airway screening, CBCT imaging, initial
education, referral for whole-system evaluation.

**Phase 2 — Whole-System Evaluation (DC).** Posture (CBP), pelvis & SIJ
(SOT/AK), cranial mechanics (CFR/BNS), foot-to-spine load transfer (Foot
Levelers).

**Phase 3 — Targeted DC Services (Right Time, Right Dose).** Structural
correction, neuromechanical organization, cranial airway facilitation, load
redistribution.

**Phase 4 — Appliance Co-Selection (DDS + DC).** Appliance chosen for both
dental tolerance and biomechanical consequence; manufacturer guidance applied in
biomechanical context.

**Phase 5 — Appliance Delivery (DDS).**

**Phase 6 — Reassessment & Iteration (DC).** Evaluate compensation, adjust the
plan, feed biomechanical data back to the DDS and manufacturer.

The DC is not a step in someone else's process. The DC is the **thread** that
runs through all six phases.

## Why This Is Chiropractic's Opportunity — Not a Turf War

CSA's stance toward dentistry and industry is **collaboration, not conquest:**

- We do **not** sell devices.
- We do **not** diagnose within the dentist's scope or the physician's scope.
- We do **not** compete with dentists — we complete the picture around them.

What chiropractic brings is the biomechanical lens: cervical loading, sagittal
balance, cranial strain, pelvic compensation, ground reaction forces. When
manufacturers and dentists understand these, devices are chosen and placed
better. That is a gift the profession can offer — a reason for the field to
respect chiropractic, not fear it.

CSA proposes a **bidirectional education loop:** manufacturers and dentists teach
us device mechanics and dental scope; chiropractic teaches biomechanical
consequence. Everyone's outcomes improve.

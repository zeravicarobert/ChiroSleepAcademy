## Disclaimers
This workbook is **education for Doctors of Chiropractic.** It does not diagnose,
treat, or prescribe for any specific disease, and it is not individualized medical
advice. Sleep studies are interpreted by a licensed physician; the DC's role is
structural evaluation, education, coordination, and — where a given state's scope
allows — clinical interpretation within that scope. Moderate-to-severe
sleep-disordered breathing is **co-managed** with the appropriate medical
provider. Nothing here positions structural care as a replacement for CPAP, oral
appliances, surgery, or medication, and no patient is ever advised to stop
prescribed therapy.

# Part I — Foundations of Structural Airway Chiropractic

## Ch 1 — History & Philosophy
Chiropractic began in the late 19th century as a drug-free system built on the
principle that **structure governs function.** D.D. Palmer founded it in an era
rich with anatomy, neurology, and vitalism; A.T. Still founded osteopathy in the
same period. Both sought to restore the frame and the motion of the body's fluids
and forces. CSA is **chiropractic** — it studies the whole body and freely learns
from any tradition, but it is not "osteopathic." California matured chiropractic
into a licensed, diagnosing profession within its defined scope; the DC combines
that scope with a structure-first view of the person.

## Ch 2 — From Dogma to Discernment (the governing method)
CSA's operating rule, stated plainly: **we do not judge truth claims; we judge
clinical consequence.** A technique earns standing by what it measurably produces,
not by the confidence of its story. Dr. Dan Murphy's teaching that unexamined
**dogma** is the profession's chief obstacle sits at the heart of this workbook:
hold strong roots, test everything, keep what works, and report honestly —
including what doesn't respond. Every model in these pages is labeled either
**established** or **hypothesis under investigation.**

## Ch 3 — Innate Intelligence & Primary Respiratory Mechanism
Innate intelligence — the body's organizing, self-healing capacity — is expressed
in measurable ways (cellular signaling, tissue adaptation, neural plasticity); we
teach it as biology, not mysticism. **PRI/PRM** (a proposed subtle cranial-sacral
rhythm) and the **Reciprocal Tension Membrane / dura** are presented as
**cranial-osteopathic models under investigation** — useful lenses, not settled
physiology. Teach them as such.

## Ch 4 — Anatomy for Airway Function (established)
- **Viscerocranium & sphenoid** — the facial skeleton and keystone bone shaping the
  airway aperture and sinus drainage.
- **Hyoid apparatus** — suspends tongue and larynx; the geniohyoid/mylohyoid/
  stylohyoid/omohyoid/sternohyoid group governs tongue posture and airway patency.
- **Mandibular plane** — a measurable indicator of craniofacial growth pattern and
  airway volume. This is solid, mainstream anatomy — the credible base of the whole
  model.

## Ch 5 — Tensegrity / Biotensegrity (🔑 CORE framing)
Tensegrity (Buckminster Fuller; sculptures by Kenneth Snelson) and its biological
application, **biotensegrity (Stephen Levin, MD)**, describe the body as a
pre-stressed, load-sharing network: bones float as compression elements inside a
continuous tension web of muscle and fascia. CSA **applies** this established
framing to the cranium and airway — *we did not invent it.* The teaching phrase:
**motion is distributed, deformation is shared; no single joint "moves" — the
system yields.** Cranial **suture** micromotion and compliance under load are real;
the *degree* of motion and its sleep/airway significance are what CSA is testing.
This is the credible replacement for "cranial pump" language.

# Part II — Sleep Science & Airway Mechanics

## Ch 6 — Upper-Airway Obstructive Sleep Apnea (UAOSA)
Mechanical collapse of the upper airway during sleep → intermittent desaturation,
micro-arousals, systemic stress. CSA's contribution is a **biomechanical lens**:
craniofacial restriction, TMJ/hyoid imbalance, forward-head posture / cervical
hypolordosis, and pelvic compensation may all be **contributors** worth assessing —
**in addition to**, not instead of, the recognized medical picture. Untreated OSA
is associated with cardiometabolic strain and cognitive effects; this is why proper
testing matters.

## Ch 7 — Sleep Homeostasis (mainstream)
Adenosine accumulates during wakefulness and builds sleep pressure. *(A proposed
CSA/Murphy hypothesis that adenosine concentrates in cranial/sphenoid structures is
**unproven** — present it only as a question to investigate, never as fact.)*

## Ch 8 — Testing & Severity (physician-read)
**Home Sleep Test (HST)** records O₂ saturation, airflow, snoring, and AHI, ordered
in collaboration with a licensed sleep lab or telemedicine provider and
**read by a physician.** AHI (AASM): Normal <5 · Mild 5–14 · Moderate 15–29 ·
Severe ≥30. The DC correlates the numbers with structural findings (posture, tone,
airway shape) within scope — the objective anchor of the case.

## Ch 9 — The Consequence Standard in Practice
Document **before and after**: posture photos, CBCT/lateral ceph airway volume, HST
metrics (AHI/ODI/SpO₂), and patient-reported outcomes. Report **responders and
non-responders.** A measurable improvement shows structure **contributed in that
case** — never a cure, never proof of mechanism. All data flows to the **CSA
Outcomes Registry.**

## Ch 10 — Report of Findings, Education & Scope
A two-day consult (history/exam/test review → education/plan) builds awareness and
commitment. Core scope rule: **sleep and airway are biomechanical problems that
cross licensure lines; CSA is where those lines collaborate without collapsing
scope.** Screen, coordinate testing, educate, co-manage, refer.

# Part III — VAET™ as a Framework

## Ch 11 — What VAET Is
VAET is **not a new cranial technique** but a **condition-specific application
framework** that sequences established methods (CFR/BNS, SOT, AK, CBP,
postural-foundation work) for the airway indication, in a defined order and
time-limited window. Its aim: support airway aperture, fluid exchange, and
neuromuscular tone. VAET is offered as a legitimate **early structural
intervention, honestly framed** — always measured (pre/post HST), co-managed, and
**never** positioned as superior to or a replacement for CPAP, appliances, or
surgery.

## Ch 12 — CFR / BNS (with safety)
CFR: a balloon-instrument procedure mobilizing cranial sutures via the nasal
passages (Dr. Z certified since 2012), applied within a full-body structural model.
BNS: a related nasal/cranial method. **Safety:** sterile nasal balloons only;
**contraindications** include recent nasal surgery, severe sinus infection, and
uncontrolled hypertension; obtain **informed consent** and document nasal patency
pre/post.

## Ch 13 — SOT, CBP & Postural Foundation
SOT pelvic categories I–III (craniosacral respiration; SI weight transfer; disc/
dural fixation) and CBP cervical-lordosis correction are integrated and **measured**
(e.g., pre/post cervical angles). Any claim that lordosis change reduces AHI is a
**hypothesis to test per case**, reported honestly — not a promised result.

## Ch 14 — Cerebrospinal Fluid
Teach it precisely: **CSF exchange is rhythmic and sleep-enhanced** (pulsatile,
glymphatic — established). Whether **cranial mechanics assist** that exchange is
**CSA's model under investigation.** State the two clauses separately; do not fuse
them into one asserted fact. *(Retire "Liquid Light" and "trampoline effect" from
professional copy — vivid internal metaphors only, never presented as physiology.)*

## Ch 15 — Documentation
SOAP notes; VAET session findings; pre/post metrics (posture, airway volume, HST,
symptom survey); re-evaluation timeline. Documentation is what converts belief into
evidence.

# Part IV — Whole-Person Support (evidence-based only)

# Part V — Applied Clinical Protocols

## Ch 17 — Screening & Patient Education
Mallampati class as an **education/communication tool (not a diagnostic test)**;
snoring/witnessed-apnea logs; STOP-BANG/Epworth screening; the structural airway
exam (Mallampati, tongue scalloping, forward-head, cranial-postural symmetry,
**asymmetric palatal yield** as a documented observation to correlate — not a
diagnosis).

## Ch 18 — Case Documentation & the Registry
De-identified case records (Case IDs, no PHI in shared trackers), outcomes reported
in full including non-responders, feeding the CSA Outcomes Registry — the honest
evidence base that earns the profession's respect.

## Ch 19 — Scope & Referral Logic
When to screen, when to test, when to co-manage, when to refer (MD/ENT/DDS). "Every
provider stays in their lane; CSA coordinates." Moderate-severe → medical
co-management.

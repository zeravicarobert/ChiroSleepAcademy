## 1. Why this workshop, for this room

The Liberpulco audience is not chiropractors. It's voluntaryists, crypto and
decentralization people, entrepreneurs, and self-sovereignty builders. They
care about **owning their own stuff** — their keys, their money, their data,
their choices — and about not outsourcing their lives to institutions they
didn't consent to.

That is the bridge. The pitch is not "let me teach you about chiropractic."
The pitch is:

> **Your body is the one piece of sovereign territory you can never leave.
> Most people have quietly handed their sleep and their breathing over to a
> system they don't control. Here's how to take it back — structurally, from
> the skull down.**

Sleep and airway is the perfect topic for this crowd because it's:

- **Personal sovereignty made physical.** You can't decentralize your life if
  you're exhausted, foggy, and running on 6 broken hours.
- **A decentralization story.** Home sleep testing (HST) moved the sleep lab
  out of the hospital and into your bedroom. That's *their* kind of story:
  taking a gatekept institution and putting it in the individual's hands.
- **Anti-fragility, not anti-medicine.** We are pro–taking ownership, not
  anti-CPAP, anti-dentist, or anti-doctor. Sovereignty means having the tools
  and the knowledge to make your own informed calls — and knowing when to
  bring in a specialist you chose.

## 3. Credibility guardrails (read before every rehearsal)

Per CSA red lines (CLAUDE.md §5) — these do not bend for a liberty crowd, and
in fact this audience respects them *more*:

- **No cure claims.** VAET and chiropractic do not cure sleep apnea. We correct
  structure and support the airway; we do not promise to fix OSA.
- **No attacks.** Do not trash CPAP, oral appliances, surgery, drugs, dentists,
  ENTs, MDs, or sleep medicine. A self-sovereignty room will *love* an
  anti-establishment rant — do not take the bait. Staying respectful is what
  separates a credible doctor from a hype merchant.
- **DCs don't diagnose OSA.** The role is structural. HST data is read by a
  qualified sleep physician; the chiropractor's job is structure, education,
  and coordination.
- **No fear-based marketing, no hype, no internet-bro energy.** Doctor-teacher
  voice, even on a liberty stage. Calm authority beats volume.
- **Honest about evidence.** Say plainly what's proven and what's still being
  proven.

The move: channel the room's anti-institutional energy into **personal
ownership and informed choice**, never into "the doctors are lying to you."

## 5. The self-audit block (the part that makes the workshop)

Give every attendee a one-page scorecard (build as a separate handout, see
Next Steps). Walk them through scoring themselves live. People remember the
workshop where *they got their own number*, not the one where they watched
slides.

Components:

1. **STOP-BANG** (snoring, tiredness, observed apnea, pressure, BMI, age, neck,
   gender) — quick apnea-risk screen. Frame: "this is a self-audit, not a
   diagnosis."
2. **Epworth Sleepiness Scale** — daytime sleepiness score.
3. **Mallampati self-check** — mirror or phone selfie, open the mouth, see how
   much of the throat is visible. Airway real estate, made visible.
4. **Nasal-breathing / mouth-breathing check** — a simple daytime awareness
   test + the idea of nasal breathing as the default sovereign setting.
5. **The one honest sentence:** "If you scored high, that's not a diagnosis —
   it's a signal to get objective data (an HST) and to look at your structure."

Guardrail: every score is framed as **information for the individual to act on**,
never as CSA telling them they have a disease.

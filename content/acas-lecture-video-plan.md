## Format decision — "Keynote with cutaways"

**Robert on camera + slides visible.** Three workable setups, in
order of production cost:

| Tier | Setup | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | Robert seated/standing in front of an LED TV displaying slides. Single camera. | Fast. Can shoot tonight. Looks like a webinar. | Lower production value. Tight framing. |
| **Standard ⭐ recommended** | Robert in foreground, slides projected/displayed behind. A-cam wide, B-cam tight. Lav mic. | Looks like a TED-style talk. Reusable as keynote, sales asset, and CEU replay. | Needs 2 cameras + sound + space. |
| **Premium** | Green-screen Robert, slides composited in post. Multi-cam. | Best look. Editable after. | Way more edit time. Panos may not be ready. |

**Recommendation:** Standard tier. Achievable in the San Diego
AirBnB with Santiago on A-cam, Brayson on B-cam (handheld or
tripod), Panos on sound + monitor.

---

I entered chiropractic like everyone else — through the spine.

I was taught that chiropractic began and ended with musculoskeletal complaints:
posture, pain, rehab, insurance protocols. For years I practiced as a
traditional DC — adjusting spines, stretching muscles, helping people feel
better. But I kept sensing something was missing.

Then I started seeing patterns the textbooks never explained:

- Neck pain that wasn't really about the neck.
- Back pain that wasn't really about the back.
- TMJ that wasn't really about the jaw.
- Migraines that weren't really about the head.

Again and again, these carried an upstream story — in sleep, in the airway, in
the rhythm of breathing and cranial motion — long before they showed up as
spinal problems.

The more I studied AK, SOT, cranial work, CFR, dura mechanics, airway
physiology, and sleep science, the clearer it became: chiropractic isn't only
musculoskeletal. It's also respiratory, neurological, and rhythmic — and it
often starts at the skull.

Here's the idea I couldn't unsee: many spinal problems begin upstream. Misaligned
cranial structure, a collapsed airway, restricted breathing, disrupted sleep —
these can drive the very downstream misalignments DCs adjust all day.

So I began to rebuild how I practice — from the top down.
Not spine-first. Not pain-first.
**Skull-first. Sleep-first. Airway-first. Biomechanics-first.**

I started teaching patients — and then doctors — that alignment begins with the
rhythm of breath, the motion of the cranial bones, the tension of the dura, the
aperture of the airway, and the neurological patterns that govern everything
below.

For too long, chiropractors have been boxed into rehab and musculoskeletal care
— a box that's too small. We're more than "pain doctors" or "alternative PTs."
We align the body from skull to feet, and we have a **real, missing role in
sleep and airway health.**

So now I re-teach chiropractic to practicing DCs the way I wish I'd learned it —
starting at the skull, supporting sleep, addressing the airway, and rebuilding
alignment the way the body actually works, top to bottom.

This is my story. This is my mission. And this is the new category of
chiropractic I want to help give back to the profession.

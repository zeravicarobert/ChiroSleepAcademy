## Introduction — The Playground
> A new kid joins the school playground. The dentist and the chiropractor both want
> to help. Someone has to explain the rules of the playground.

This workbook explains:
- what the playground is (sleep apnea care)
- who sets the rules (biomechanics + scope)
- how dentists can **help more patients in less chair time**
- without turf wars, over-education, or wasted effort

**(For dentists):**
> You don't have to run the playground. You just need to know where to stand.

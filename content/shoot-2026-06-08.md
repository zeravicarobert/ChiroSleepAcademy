## BLOCK 3 — THE CLINICAL CORE (25-35 min)

**Setup:** Whiteboard preferred. Robert draws as he teaches.

### Topics in order

#### Skull anatomy series
- [ ] **The skull is an extremity** — most don't know this. Reframe
      it on camera.
- [ ] **Each bone of the skull** — Robert teaches each with
      anatomical name + function. Crew capture the close-up of his
      hands pointing at the model/diagram/whiteboard.
  - Frontal, parietals, temporals, occipital, sphenoid, ethmoid
  - Maxilla, palatine, zygomatic, lacrimal, nasal, inferior nasal
    conchae, vomer, mandible
- [ ] **Viscerocranium vs. Neurocranium** — define the two parts
  - **Neurocranium** = the brain case (8 bones)
  - **Viscerocranium** = the face (14 bones)
- [ ] **The two parts make a PUMP** — together they create the
      cranial mechanism that pumps cerebrospinal fluid

#### CSF and the cranial-sacral pump
- [ ] **CSF — what it is, where it's made, where it flows**
  - ⚠️ Production correction (from CLAUDE.md content/README.md):
        primarily LATERAL ventricles via choroid plexus, exits via
        4th ventricle through foramina of Magendie + Luschka
- [ ] **The brake-fluid analogy** — CSF is a constant-pressure
      tubular system, just like an automotive brake line
- [ ] **The cranial-sacral pump as the original respiratory
      mechanism** — *"Before you had lungs, you had a pump in your
      skull and sacrum that moved fluid that fed your brain. That
      pump never stops working."* (Foundational SOT/PRI/PRM
      teaching from Robert's lineage.)
- [ ] **Why this pump matters for sleep + airway** — if cranial
      mechanism is restricted, fluid moves wrong, structure
      compresses, airway suffers

#### The poetic frame (Robert's signature)
- [ ] **CSF and light** — *"CSF behaves like a light-carrying
      medium. Subluxation reads as low light. When I correct
      structure, light returns to the system."* (Soften from "glows
      in the dark" pending source verification.)
- [ ] Connect to **ADIO** (Above-Down-Inside-Out) — classical
      chiropractic vitalistic principle. Above (skull, brain) →
      down (cord, body) → inside (innate) → out (expression).

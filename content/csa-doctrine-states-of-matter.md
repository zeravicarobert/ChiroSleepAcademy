## Chiropractic Sleep Academy — Doctrine

Chiropractic operates across the **three states of matter** — solid, liquid, and
gas — each requiring different tools, training, and delivery models.

### Chiropractic 1.0 — Solids (Structure)
Solids-based chiropractic addresses bones, joints, posture, and mechanical
alignment through in-office care. Diversified, CBP, and orthotic-based practices
excel here. This work is foundational and essential.

### Chiropractic 2.0 — Liquids (Flow)
Liquids-based chiropractic addresses cerebrospinal fluid dynamics, cranial-sacral
pumping, pelvic organization, and neuromechanical coordination. Techniques such as
SOT, Applied Kinesiology cranials, and VAET support pressure, flow, and
adaptability. This work is intensive and specialized.

### Chiropractic 3.0 — Gas (Airway / Breath)
Gas-based chiropractic addresses sleep, airway, and breathing — which govern how
the body finds its **rhythm, recovers, adapts, and regulates** over time. Delivered
through telehealth and education. **CSA certifies DCs to work at this level.**

> CSA exists to **coordinate — not replace** — Chiropractic 1.0 and 2.0 care. Its
> mission is to restore sleep and airway function so structural and cranial work
> can succeed.

## Why this wording works
- **Scientifically clean.** Solid / liquid / **gas** are the three real states of
  matter — an MD or scientist won't stumble on "three states of matter" the way
  they would with *energy* as the third.
- **Gas = air, breath, airway** — the literal, perfect fit for a sleep-and-airway
  academy. CSA = the **Gas / airway / breath** level of the profession → *CSA is
  Chiropractic 3.0.*
- Ties to the **Breathing Distinction** (`csa-framework.md`): "a body breathes."
- **"Coordinate, not replace"** matches the white papers' collaborative stance.

## Guardrails
- **Rhythm / recovery / regulation** is what the Gas level *governs* — not a
  separate "Energy" tier. This keeps three true states of matter and **retires the
  old "energy = frequencies/vibrations" risk** (CLAUDE.md §18) from all
  clinical/MD/patient copy. *(If Robert later wants a distinct 4th "Energy" tier,
  revisit — but note the 4th real state is plasma, not energy.)*
- **Two ladders stay separate.** This is the **Chiropractic X.0** ladder (the
  profession). The **CSA X.0** curriculum ladder is different (1.0 Anatomy &
  Physiology → 2.0 Practice Management → 3.0 Sales & Closing). Always say
  "Chiropractic 1.0/2.0/3.0" here — never "CSA 1.0/2.0/3.0."
- **Hypothesis-level:** CSF dynamics and cranial-sacral pumping (2.0) are CSA's
  model under investigation, not settled fact — teach them that way.
- **"Support," not "restore"** (retired verb, §18).
- **VAET** sits in 2.0 (Liquids) here for its fluid/cranial mechanics, but its
  clinical *aim* is airway (3.0/Gas). Not a contradiction — VAET bridges fluid and
  gas — but keep its primary home consistent when you teach it.

## Chiropractic Sleep Academy — Doctrine

Chiropractic operates across three states of matter, each requiring different
tools, training, and delivery models.

### Chiropractic 1.0 — Solids (Structure)
Solids-based chiropractic addresses bones, joints, posture, and mechanical
alignment through in-office care. Diversified, CBP, and orthotic-based practices
excel here. This work is foundational and essential.

### Chiropractic 2.0 — Liquids (Flow)
Liquids-based chiropractic addresses cerebrospinal fluid dynamics, cranial-sacral
pumping, pelvic organization, and neuromechanical coordination. Techniques such as
SOT, Applied Kinesiology cranials, and VAET restore pressure, flow, and
adaptability. This work is intensive and specialized.

### Chiropractic 3.0 — Energy (Rhythm)
Energy-based chiropractic addresses sleep, airway, circadian rhythm, and
regulation. Delivered through telehealth and education, it governs how the body
recovers, adapts, and heals over time. CSA certifies DCs to work at this level.

> CSA exists to **coordinate — not replace** — Chiropractic 1.0 and 2.0 care. Its
> mission is to restore sleep and airway function so structural and cranial work
> can succeed.

## Editor's notes — what's strong, and three flags

**What's strong:**
- The states-of-matter metaphor is **memorable and teachable.**
- It **maps cleanly onto the existing Chiropractic X.0 ladder** (CLAUDE.md §18):
  1.0 spine → 2.0 spine+skull/cranial → 3.0 telehealth. This doctrine is basically
  that same ladder with a vivid new label — so it's compatible, not a contradiction.
- **"Coordinate, not replace"** is exactly the collaborative positioning of the
  white papers. On-brand.

**Three flags before it's canon:**

1. **⚠️ "Energy" is not a state of matter.** The three states are solid, liquid,
   **gas** (then plasma). An MD or scientist will catch "three states of matter"
   with *energy* as the third. Two clean fixes:
   - Call it **"three domains"** (solid, liquid, energy) — drops the physics claim,
     keeps the metaphor; **or**
   - Use **Gas** for the third — and *gas literally fits the airway* (air, breath,
     oxygen). You could even run **four**: Solids → Liquids → **Gas (airway/breath)**
     → Energy (rhythm/regulation). Worth considering — gas is the most on-point
     state for a sleep-and-airway academy.

2. **⚠️ Lock the meaning of "Energy."** As you define it here — *rhythm, circadian,
   sleep, regulation* — it's the **safe, defensible** reading (autonomic /
   metabolic / recovery). Keep it defined exactly that way. Do **not** let "energy"
   drift back to *"frequencies and vibrations"* — that's your own §18 highest
   credibility risk, flagged to stay out of clinical/MD copy. As written here,
   you're on the right side of the line. Guard it.

3. **Two ladders — don't merge them.** This doctrine is the **Chiropractic X.0**
   ladder (the profession). Keep it separate from the **CSA X.0 curriculum** ladder
   (1.0 Anatomy & Physiology → 2.0 Practice Management → 3.0 Sales & Closing =
   website pillars). Same numbers, different ladders (CLAUDE.md §18). This doctrine
   should say "Chiropractic 1.0/2.0/3.0," never "CSA 1.0/2.0/3.0."

**Minor:**
- *"restore pressure, flow"* — "restore" is a retired verb (§18); prefer
  *support / establish / improve*.
- CSF dynamics and cranial-sacral pumping are **hypothesis-level** — teach as
  CSA's model under investigation, not settled fact.
- **VAET placement:** here it's under 2.0 (Liquids). Elsewhere VAET is the Level-3
  *clinical method* under the Unsubluxated Structural Breathing System, and its
  work is airway (which is 3.0 here). Not wrong — VAET touches both fluid and
  airway — but decide the primary home so the story stays clean.

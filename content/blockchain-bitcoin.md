## Resources

### WhatsOnChain — https://whatsonchain.com/

- **What it is:** A blockchain explorer for the BSV (Bitcoin SV) network —
  lets you look up blocks, transactions, and addresses, view on-chain data,
  mempool and network stats, and access the same data through a REST API.
- **Why it's here:** Recommended by Eleanor Wilson (new to me — see index below).
- **How it might be used:** Independent verification of on-chain transactions
  and addresses; pulling on-chain data/stats via its API. Free to use up to
  rate limits, with paid tiers for heavier usage.
- **Added:** 2026-07-27

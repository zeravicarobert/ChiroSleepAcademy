# Chiropractic Sleep Academy — Hat Names (Org / Accountability Chart)

**Robert-authored, 2026-07-16.** The "hats" (roles/functions) CSA runs on. A hat
is a *job to be done*, not a person — early on **one person wears many hats**
(right now that's mostly Robert). This is the target structure to delegate into
over time. Ties to `partnership/rob-peter-csa-agreement.md` and
`content/strategy-renting-credibility.md`.

---

## EXECUTIVE
- **Founder & Director** — vision, IP, final say, the CSA name/authority.
- **Policy Officer** — the rules, red lines, standards, and "what we will / won't do."

## COMMAND
- **Operations & Continuity Anchor** — keeps the machine running day to day; nothing
  falls through.
- **Education & Curriculum Coordinator** — the workbook, lectures, curriculum ladder.
- **Marketing & Authority Distribution Lead** — content, outreach, getting the
  authority in front of the market.
- **Referral & Network Manager** — DDS/MD/ENT/HST/CBCT relationships and the
  provider network.
- **Compliance & Documentation Steward** — consent, HIPAA, legal docs, records,
  keeping everything defensible.

## PRODUCTION
- **Enrollment & Member Services Coordinator** — sign-ups, onboarding, member care.
- **Media & Content Librarian** — files, assets, the knowledge base, version control.
- **Events & Workshop Coordinator** — seminars, Tecopa, logistics.
- **Systems & Technology Steward** — ClickFunnels, Drive, trackers, tools.

---

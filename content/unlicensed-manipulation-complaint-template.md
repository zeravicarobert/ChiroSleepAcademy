# Complaint Template — Unlicensed Spinal Manipulation (California)

**Saved 2026-07-17.** A reusable, fill-in-the-blanks complaint for reporting a
non-licensee performing spinal manipulation/adjustment (HVLA thrust) in California.
File it with the right body (below). **This is a general template, not legal advice —
report only what you personally, truthfully observed, and confirm the current filing
process on each agency's site.**

> **The core violation is the *unlicensed manipulation itself*** — performing
> chiropractic (a spinal adjustment/HVLA thrust) without a license. Lack of history,
> screening, or imaging is a **safety** point layered on top, not the core violation.

## Where to file (pick the right door — or file multiple)
1. **California Board of Chiropractic Examiners (BCE)** — unlicensed practice of
   chiropractic. They can pursue cease-and-desist and refer for prosecution.
2. **California Massage Therapy Council (CAMTC)** — if the person is a certified
   massage therapist acting outside massage scope (adjusting is outside CMT scope).
   They can discipline/de-certify.
3. **District Attorney / City Attorney** — unlicensed practice can be charged as a
   misdemeanor; a documented packet is powerful.
4. **(Systemic/chain patterns)** California Attorney General.

## Notes for Robert
- **Firsthand + specific wins.** "I saw an HVLA thrust to the thoracic spine with an
  audible cavitation on [date] at [place]" beats "they adjust people." Details make it
  actionable.
- **Evidence helps** — a photo of the ad/chair, a website printout advertising
  "adjustments," a business card.
- **Regulators/prosecutors do the enforcing** — you feed them; they act. That keeps
  *you* out of defamation exposure.
- **The private-lawsuit door** (UCL, Bus. & Prof. Code §17200) is separate and needs a
  California attorney (standing/injury). This template is for the regulatory/criminal
  channels, which are the realistic first move.

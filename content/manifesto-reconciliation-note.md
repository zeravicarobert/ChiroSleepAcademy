# Reconciliation Note — The Two Manifestos

*Internal note. Not public copy. Captures how Dr. Zeravica's two manifestos
relate so future sessions and decisions stay consistent.*

## The two documents

1. **A Christian Chiropractic Manifesto** (Google Drive, Dr. Zeravica, DC)
   — a Biblical revision of R.W. Stephenson's 33 Chiropractic Principles (1927),
   KJV scripture throughout. Replaces the pantheistic "Universal Intelligence"
   with God as Creator and sustainer. The **theological foundation.**

2. **The Structural Airway Manifesto** (`content/structural-airway-manifesto.md`)
   — the market-facing declaration of Structural Airway Chiropractic for the
   Liberpulco / health-freedom audience. Lightly spiritual, not scripture-heavy.
   The **public face.**

## Relationship: foundation and face

They are **not meant to merge.** Two audiences, one man. The Christian manifesto
is *why the body heals* (for a faith audience); the Structural Airway manifesto
is *what we're building* (for a broader, sovereignty-minded audience). The rule:
they must never **contradict**, even though they never fully **overlap**.

## Shared anchors (already consistent)

- **Genesis 2:7 — the breath of life** anchors both (Christian Principles 2 & 18;
  the Structural Airway deck's scriptural hook and the manifesto's opening).
- **Restore** — "I will restore health unto thee" (Jer 30:17, Christian
  Principle 23) ↔ "Restore the structure. Restore the breath."
- **Alignment = health, interference = dysfunction** — subluxation/interference
  (Christian Principles 12, 17, 30, 31) ↔ "the structure you breathe through /
  the kink."
- **Honesty about limits** — the fallen world; ultimate restoration in eternity,
  not this life (Christian Principles 5, 24, 25) ↔ "no cure claims; honest about
  what is proven." The theology and the ethics say the same thing.

## The one tension — resolved

- **Tension:** the public manifesto originally said breath is "the most personal
  thing **you own**." The Christian manifesto says "ye are **not your own**; for
  ye are bought with a price" (1 Cor 6:19–20, Principle 23).
- **Resolution — stewardship language.** Public manifesto now reads: breath "was
  the first gift you were given… the most personal thing you are responsible
  for… stewardship of the one thing you can never hand to someone else." This
  keeps the self-responsibility punch the Liberpulco audience responds to
  (nobody else is responsible for your body — not the system) while honoring the
  conviction that we did not author the design.

## Guidance for future use

- Keep the public manifesto **lightly spiritual** — do not import KJV scripture
  into it. The two-audience split is intentional.
- When a **Christian DC audience** is in the room, the Christian manifesto is the
  right artifact; when it's Liberpulco / partners / patients, use the Structural
  Airway manifesto.
- The bridge line, if ever asked how faith relates to the work: *the body is
  fearfully and wonderfully made, and our work restores function within a design
  we did not author.*
- Any future edit to either manifesto should be checked against the other for the
  ownership/stewardship tension and the honesty-about-limits stance.

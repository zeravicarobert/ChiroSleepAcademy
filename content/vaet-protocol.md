**VAET = Viscerocranial Airway Expansion Therapy.** It is Dr. Z's
proprietary, condition-specific **clinical protocol** for what he calls
**UAOSA** (Upper-Airway Obstructive Sleep Apnea — his own term for the
obstructive presentation he works with).

Two things must be held together to describe VAET honestly, because Dr. Z's
own description of it **evolved**:

- **Origin (2024): a reframed adjustment.** VAET began as a rebranding of
  **Cranial Facial Release (CFR)**. Dr. Z moved the emphasis away from

  viscerocranium's aperture for airflow**," and named the result
  Viscerocranial Airway Expansion Therapy. In this earliest form he also

- **Maturation (Jan 2026): a sequencing framework, not a technique.** By his
  most recent transcript, Dr. Z is emphatic that VAET does **not** replace or
  compete with CFR or BNS. VAET is the **protocol, sequence, and time-limited
  treatment window** *within which* established techniques are applied in the
  right order for the airway indication. His metaphor: the techniques are the
  instruments; VAET is the composition. His motto: *"operating at the protocol
  level, not the technique ego level."* **[H]**

**Working definition (Dr. Z's own, Jan 2026, lightly cleaned):**

## 3. The VAET Treatment Sequence (what actually happens)

Reconstructed as concretely as the chats allow. Session-level hands-on detail
is **thin** in the transcripts; the strongest specifics come from the
"Chiropractic airway expansion" chat and are marked accordingly.

**Overall pathway (matches §8 CSA pathway):**
> Tele-screen → HST (DC clinical read + diagnosis) → 2-day VAET block → post-testing → plan.

**Step A — Screening / consultation.** ~15-minute telemedicine screen: sleep
history (snoring, witnessed apnea, gasping, daytime sleepiness), CPAP /
oral-appliance / prior-testing history, TMJ/bruxism, plus a visual
cranial-postural screen (Mallampati, tongue scalloping, forward-head posture,
frontal/zygomatic/TMJ/occipital symmetry). Epworth used for daytime sleepiness.
**[T]**

**Step B — Baseline testing.** Home Sleep Test (Dr. Z references a two-night
test in places, single elsewhere), plus Epworth and a lateral posture photo,
captured **before** treatment. **HST is ordered/coordinated by CSA but read by a
licensed physician; the DC does not read the study or diagnose OSA** (see §6).
**[T]**

**Step C — The 2-day VAET block.** Dr. Z's recurring structure is **four
sessions across two days (AM and PM each day).** Each session applies the
integrated techniques in sequence: **[T]**
1. **Viscerocranial / cranial work (CFR-BNS)** — the "expansion" and drainage
  target on the viscerocranium. **[T]** technique / **[H]** stated effect.
2. **TMJ release sequence** — jaw/head/neck. **[T]**
3. **Cervical mechanics (CBP)** — hypolordosis traction, cervical-neutral
  posture. **[T]**
4. **SOT pelvic-category correction** — the caudal "other end" of the chain.
  **[T]**
5. **Postural foundation** — foot levelers / custom orthotics where indicated.
  **[T]**
- **AK muscle-testing** is used throughout to decide which of the Triad domains
  to address and to re-check change. **[T]** method.

**Retreat setting (Tecopa).** The 2-day block is the intended immersive
"Tecopa Test-Adjustment / VAET Experience": Tecopa, CA, hot-springs/retreat
context, multiple encounters over two treatment days, pre/post testing,
CFR/BNS/VAET procedures, and follow-up planning. Clinical fee ~$3,500;
all-in ~$5,500 with travel/lodging (per §8). Final schedule not locked. **[H]/TBD**

**Step D — Post-VAET bodywork (aftercare, not prep).** Dr. Z is explicit:
bodyworkers come **after** his correction to help the body *hold* it — never to
prepare the patient. Chain of command: **Dr. Z owns diagnosis-adjacent
structural evaluation, VAET, and cranial correction; bodyworkers do post-VAET
soft-tissue "band" integration** along feet → legs → pelvis → diaphragm → ribs
→ shoulders → neck, and must **avoid** cranium, palate, TMJ, intraoral work,
cervical adjusting, and any diagnosis or medical claims. **[T]**

**Step E — Post-testing & plan.** Repeat HST (~2 weeks post in his notes) plus
repeat Epworth and patient-reported outcomes; then an ongoing structural plan
and interdisciplinary referral as appropriate. **[T]**

## 4. What Is Measured

Objective before/after data is central to CSA's honesty claim. Pre/post
measures Dr. Z and the Master Summary name:

- **AHI** (apnea–hypopnea index) — baseline → post. **[T]** (primary)
- **RDI** (respiratory disturbance index). **[T]**
- **ODI** (oxygen-desaturation index) and **oxygen nadir / time below O2
  thresholds**. **[T]**
- **SpO2** (oxygen saturation). **[T]**
- **Epworth Sleepiness Scale** and patient-satisfaction / patient-reported
  outcomes. **[T]**
- **Snoring frequency/intensity**, resting respiratory rate, breath-hold time,
  sleep-stage distribution where available. **[T]**
- Optional imaging: **CBCT** airway/cranial measurement (CSA Step 3). **[T]**

Dr. Z's stated study target is a **≥20% AHI reduction** rate across cases plus
average Epworth change — framed as *measurement to learn*, not as a promised
outcome. All measured data flows to the **CSA Outcomes Registry** (Master
Summary Level 5). **[T]**

## 6. ⚠️ Gaps, Ambiguities & Contradictions to Resolve

**This is the most important section.** Each item is something Dr. Z described
inconsistently or left undefined across the chats. Robert should resolve these
before VAET is standardized.

1. **Technique vs. protocol — a genuine reversal.** In 2024 VAET *is* the
  adjustment (a rebrand of CFR: "Viscerocranial Adjustment"). By Jan 2026 VAET
  is explicitly **not** a technique but a sequencing framework that *contains*
  CFR/BNS. Both framings appear in his own words. Which is canonical? (This
  drives IP, teaching, and regulator language.)

2. ✅ **RESOLVED (2026-07-16) — Early structural-first, honestly framed.**
  Robert's decision: VAET is positioned as a legitimate *early* structural
  intervention, often appropriate to try first for suitable cases, but always
  measured, co-managed with MD/ENT, with **no cure claim**, **no "superior to /
  instead of CPAP/appliances,"** and **no "treat without drugs or machines."**
  The raw *"VAET for UAOSA 1st, not CPAPs"* / *"without drugs or machines"*
  language (§7 quotes 5) is **retired** and any copy carrying it must be
  rewritten. This keeps the structural-first conviction while staying inside the
  red lines, chiropractic scope, and advertising law.

3. **Does VAET physically expand bone, or release tension?** The shift from
  "dural tension release" to "airway aperture **expansion**" was made by Dr. Z
  as a *marketing/positioning* choice, not on new evidence. Whether VAET claims
  actual bony/aperture enlargement (measurable on CBCT) or soft-tissue/tension
  change is unsettled — and materially affects what can be claimed.

4. **CSF / sinus "drainage" mechanism.** Presented as fact ("allows drainage of
  CSF and post-nasal drip"). No measurement or citation supports it. Keep as
  hypothesis or drop.

5. **Naming instability.** Across chats the concept is called VAET,
  Viscerocranial Adjustment, Viscerocranial Airway Expansion (VAE),
  Viscerocranial Respiratory Tension Release, "Structural Airway Medicine,"
  "Cranial Sacral Respiration of the Brain," and "Human Breathing
  Infrastructure." Separately, the Master Summary's master concept
  **"Unsubluxated Structural Breathing System"** does **not appear in any of
  these transcripts.** The category/brand ladder needs one canonical set.

6. **"UAOSA" is a nonstandard term.** Dr. Z coined "Upper-Airway Obstructive
  Sleep Apnea." Standard literature says OSA. Decide whether to keep UAOSA
  (and define it) or align to OSA to avoid confusing clinicians.

7. **Session structure not fully locked.** "Four sessions over two days
  (AM/PM)" is his most consistent figure, but the Master Summary also lists
  past concepts (4-day, 2-day/4-visit, 2-day) and "multiple encounters."
  Exact sessions/day, duration, and Tecopa schedule are undefined.

8. **HST nights.** Two-night vs single-night HST is stated inconsistently.

9. **Scope of the $3,500 / $5,500 offer.** What is *in* VAET proper vs. à la
  carte (ChiroThin, orthotics, CBCT, bloodwork, bodywork, extra days) is not
  pinned down and overlaps the §8 pricing exclusions.

10. **Is the "Triad wrap" part of VAET?** ChiroThin (biochemical), NET/Bach
   Flower/homeopathy (mental-emotional), and the faith/NEW START framing are
   sometimes described as *inside* VAET (via the AK Triad of Health) and
   sometimes as separate lifestyle add-ons. This must be decided — and the
   red lines argue for keeping the spiritual/vibrational elements out of
   clinical claims entirely.

11. **Undefined clinical safety scaffolding.** No contraindications,
   inclusion/exclusion criteria, red-flag/referral triggers, "responder"
   definition, treatment-failure definition, or follow-up intervals are
   specified anywhere (Master Summary §21 confirms these are open).

12. **BNS is named but never described.** "CFR/BNS" is treated as a unit, yet
   no BNS procedure, indication, or sequence appears. Needs definition.

13. **Reproducibility.** Everything is described in Dr. Z's hands. Nothing in
   the transcripts defines how a trained DC would reproduce the sequence
   measurably — a stated CSA risk (Master Summary §22).

---

Two short ways to explain what VAET does. Same idea, two audiences.

**Patient-facing (clear, non-technical):**
> Sleep apnea places a hidden load on the body every night. VAET works by
> reducing that load at the airway and cranial level, allowing the body to
> breathe and recover more naturally.

**Doctor-facing (professional):**
> VAET reframes sleep apnea as a parasitic biomechanical load acting through the
> viscerocranium and upper airway. Structural correction reduces the load rather
> than compensating for it.

*Why this bridge is strong:* it ties VAET straight to the **extractive /
parasitic load** framework (`content/csa-training-notes.md`) and the
**Awareness-Disruption Model** — apnea as a nightly load that structure can
reduce, not a disease VAET "cures."

*Guardrails (red lines §5):*
- **Patient version stays clean** — "reducing load," "breathe and recover more
  naturally." No cure claim, no device knock. Safe for patient/MD/DDS copy.
- **"Parasitic biomechanical load"** is the **doctor/teaching register** —
  keep it for DC-facing copy, not patient headlines. It says apnea *behaves
  like* a load (analogy, CSA's lens), never a literal medical diagnosis.

  contrast (structure vs. a device that compensates) — but keep it as *VAET's
  approach*, **not** "VAET is better than CPAP." No superiority claim (§6.2,
  now retired).
- Always paired with measurement (pre/post HST) and co-management.

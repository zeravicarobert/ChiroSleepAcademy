# Founder-Led Brand — Dr. Robert Zeravica & CSA

**For:** Dr. Robert Zeravica / Chiropractic Sleep Academy
**Created:** 2026-07-09
**Status:** Draft v1 — strategic positioning note

---

## The decision

Robert is pivoting to brand **himself** — *Dr. Robert Zeravica, D.C.,
founder of Chiropractic Sleep Academy* — not only the CSA entity, on the
thesis that **branding the founder simultaneously brands CSA.**

**This is correct**, and it's Jay Abraham's *Strategy of Preeminence*
applied at the personal level: people trust a *person* — a doctor — far
more than a logo or a program. For a **category you are creating**
(Structural Airway Chiropractic), you need a human protagonist. Categories
are led by people, not brands.

---

## Why founder-led is the right call *here* specifically

1. **DCs follow doctors, not companies.** Your buyers are chiropractors.
   They join mentors and movements led by a credible clinician-teacher —
   exactly the voice CSA is built on (CLAUDE.md §4).
2. **The offer is inherently personal.** "Heal the Healer First" *is*
   Dr. Z — you went through the pathway yourself (your own HST). The story
   only works in the first person.
3. **Category creation needs a face.** "Structural Airway Chiropractic,"
   the early→mid→late model, and the **SAO** diagnosis need an author to
   be attributed to. That author is you.
4. **Trust transfers, not the reverse.** Authority accrues to the person;
   the person lends it to the entity. A strong personal brand can launch,
   rename, or extend many CSA offers. A faceless CSA can't easily create a
   trusted doctor.

**The relationship in one line:**
> **You are the trust and the voice. CSA is the vehicle and the system.
> You are the face; CSA is the machine behind it.** Brand both — the
> person carries the authority, the entity carries the offer.

## The positioning (how to say who you are)

**Primary identity line:**
> **Dr. Robert Zeravica, D.C. — founder of Chiropractic Sleep Academy;
> the chiropractor who defined Structural Airway Chiropractic and the
> early-intervention (SAO) model of sleep-and-airway care.**

**Four brand pillars (everything you publish ladders to one of these):**
1. **The category definer** — early/middle/late intervention; the SAO
   diagnosis; "you can't skip the beginning of care."
2. **The doctor who healed himself first** — lived the pathway; credible
   because he's a patient, not just a proponent.
3. **The teacher of doctors** — mentor to DCs building a sleep-and-airway
   division; the doctor-teacher voice.
4. **The ethical bridge-builder** — structure-first, collaborative where
   safety requires it, honest about proof. (Protects §5 credibility.)

## Channels (where the personal brand lives)

- **Speaking / stage** — chiropractic and airway events (Parker, CalChiro,
  airway summits). The founder on stage *is* the funnel.
- **Long-form authority** — a signature talk, a workbook/short book, a
  named framework diagram (early→mid→late; SAO).
- **Podcast / video** — guest on airway, chiro, and health-freedom shows;
  eventually host.
- **LinkedIn + email** — doctor-to-doctor thought leadership, not hype.
- **The website question (decide):** you now have **ChiroSleepPro.com**
  *and* **ChiropracticSleepAcademy.net** (CLAUDE.md domain of record).
  A founder-led brand may also warrant a **personal site**
  (e.g., DrRobertZeravica.com) that funnels into CSA. Pick a primary and
  make the others redirect — don't split authority across three domains.

---

## Guardrails (so personal brand ≠ guru brand)

- **Doctor-teacher, never internet-bro** (§4). Preeminence is quiet
  authority, not hype.
- **Credibility over reach** (§5). No cure claims, no fear marketing, no
  attacking MDs/DDS/sleep medicine.
- **Antitrust/anti-establishment conviction stays backbone, not billboard**
  — it fuels your resolve; it is not the public message.
- **Outcome-honest** — "here's what we're proving," not "miracle."

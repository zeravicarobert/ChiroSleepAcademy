*(NEW today — Robert named it explicitly)*

### The HST hook (cold/warm/hot audience)

> *"Call Sleep Doctor. Get a Home Sleep Test delivered to your home.
> Sleep with it for two nights. When you're done, we'll go over the
> results together and act accordingly."*

This is the **front-door message**. Lower friction than any other
CSA entry. Costs the prospect ~$189 cash (or $0 with insurance per
§8). They get a real diagnostic. Robert reviews. Then the pathway
opens — or doesn't, depending on findings.

> *"Now — before you ask me, 'Dr. Z, what's this going to cost me

> stated before you commit to anything. You're never on a runaway
> escalator with me."*

This single message — recorded once — can be the **lead clip on the

## 3. Why this matters to CSA

CSA's mission is to make **Structural Airway Chiropractic** a credible,
ethical voice in the sleep-and-airway conversation (CLAUDE.md §1–2). A
legislative-advocacy community is a natural adjacency:

- **Scope & access to care.** Sleep, airway, and chiropractic scope-of-practice
  issues live in state legislatures and boards. DCs who advocate are exactly
  CSA's audience (SOT/AK/CFR/CBP, airway-aware DCs — CLAUDE.md §3).
- **Category-building is a movement.** CSA is "not just a course… a movement"
  (§1). Advocates already know how to organize, message, and show up. That's
  the same muscle CSA needs for outreach and its funnel (§10–11).
- **Warm relationships.** People who show up twice a week to advocate are
  high-commitment. They're strong candidates for CSA outreach, partnership,
  and the broadcast-channel funnel idea (§ session notes).

### Guardrails when representing CSA here (from CLAUDE.md §4–5)
- Doctor-teacher voice. No hype, no fear-based framing, no internet-bro energy.
- Never claim VAET/chiropractic cures sleep apnea; never attack CPAP, oral
  appliances, MDs, DDS, ENTs, or sleep medicine.
- CSA *adds the structural layer* to the airway conversation — collaborator,
  never competitor. Protect CSA's credibility above any single moment.

## 4. How CSA can serve this community (ideas — refine on the calls)

Draft ideas, not commitments. Robert to test which land.

1. **Show up first, serve first.** Attend consistently, learn the bills, be a
   genuine contributor before ever mentioning CSA. Earn the right.
2. **Offer the structural-airway education.** A free, on-brand teaching drop

3. **Bridge to CSA's partners.** Many of CSA's named strategic partners
   (airway-aware DDS, CE, CBCT — CLAUDE.md §15, Sal Rodas / Foundation for
   Airway Health) overlap with health-advocacy circles. Look for warm intros.
4. **Advocacy that protects CSA's model.** Track any bills touching
   chiropractic scope, HST ordering, telehealth, or airway/sleep care that
   affect whether DCs can run the CSA pathway (§7–8).
5. **Recruit serious DCs.** High-commitment advocates → candidates for the
   90-day funnel (outreach → resource → Zoom → offer) and a first cohort (§11).

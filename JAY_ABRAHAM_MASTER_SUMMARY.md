Dr. Zeravica has many legitimate opportunities:

* SDI;
* CSA;
* VAET;
* coaching;
* clinical retreats;
* DC education;
* dentist partnerships;
* sleep testing;
* licensing;
* land and property;
* consulting;
* funnels;
* veteran services;
* Nexus letters;
* data and conferences.

The unresolved tension is not whether these are valuable. It is:

> Which activities belong now, which belong later, which belong to partners, and which should remain passive assets?

## 14.4 Clinical mechanism versus cautious positioning

Dr. Zeravica does not want to "hide his light under a bushel." He wants to make strong clinical and category claims about structural breathing and biomechanics.

At the same time, legal, scientific, professional, and advertising defensibility require:

* distinguishing hypothesis from established evidence;
* avoiding claims beyond available data;
* avoiding unlawful scope expansion;
* documenting outcomes;
* using appropriate disclaimers;
* involving attorneys and research professionals.

The desired position is not silence. It is **strong, evidence-seeking, legally defensible category leadership**.

## 14.5 "Treat sleep apnea" versus structural-breathing category

The framing evolved away from merely saying that chiropractors "treat sleep apnea."

The more current framing emphasizes:

* structural breathing;
* airway biomechanics;
* early intervention;
* unsubluxating the structural breathing system;
* creating structural breathing stability;
* expanding the airway and related structures;
* integrating with—not necessarily replacing—testing, equipment, dental care, or medical care.

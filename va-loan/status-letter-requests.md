# Status-Letter Requests — Templates & How-To

How to obtain the four "proof" documents the underwriter needs. Two you pull
yourself online (IRS, FTB); two you request (child support, student loans) —
request templates below.

---

## 1. IRS payment-plan proof — pull it yourself

1. Go to **IRS.gov → Online Account** (id.me login).
2. Set up the installment agreement there (or "Online Payment Agreement") if not
   done, once the back returns are filed.
3. Download: the **agreement approval notice** *and* an **Account Transcript** for
   each year showing the balance and the active agreement.
4. After you've made payments, the transcript also shows the **payment history** —
   that's your "on-time payments" proof.

## 2. CA FTB payment-plan proof — pull it yourself

1. Go to **FTB.ca.gov → MyFTB account**.
2. Request the **installment agreement** online (after returns are filed).
3. Download the **confirmation letter** and the **payment history** from MyFTB.

---

## 3. Child support — current-status request

> Use if administered through **CA DCSS** (call your caseworker first — many
> counties issue the printout on request without a written letter). If it's a
> direct court order, address the same request to the court / State Disbursement
> Unit. Fill every `[BRACKET]`.

[DATE]

To: [CA Dept. of Child Support Services — County of ______ / Court name]
Re: Case #[CASE NUMBER] — Request for current-status / payment-history statement

To Whom It May Concern:

I am the [obligor/paying parent] on the above case. I am applying for a home loan
and my lender requires written confirmation of my child-support account status.

Please provide a statement showing:
- that my account is **current with no arrears** as of today's date,
- my **monthly support amount**, and
- a **payment history** for the past 12 months.

My details: Robert Zeravica, DOB [DOB], SSN ending [####], address [ADDRESS].
Please send to [EMAIL / ADDRESS], or let me know how to retrieve it from my online
case. Thank you.

Sincerely,
Robert Zeravica · [PHONE] · [EMAIL]

---

## 4. Student loans — current-status request

> Send to **each servicer** (or pull equivalent from StudentAid.gov for federal
> loans). Fill every `[BRACKET]`.

[DATE]

To: [SERVICER NAME]
Re: Account #[ACCOUNT NUMBER] — Request for status/verification letter

To Whom It May Concern:

I am applying for a home loan and my lender requires written verification of my
student loan status. Please provide a letter or statement confirming:
- that the account is **current and not in default**,
- the **current monthly payment amount** (or, if deferred/in forbearance, the
  terms and end date), and
- the **remaining balance**.

Borrower: Robert Zeravica, DOB [DOB], SSN ending [####].
Please send to [EMAIL / ADDRESS]. Thank you.

Sincerely,
Robert Zeravica · [PHONE] · [EMAIL]

---

### ⚠️ If a student loan is in default
Don't send the letter above — fix it first. A defaulted federal loan flags in
CAIVRS and blocks VA approval. Call the servicer / the Default Resolution Group
and start **loan rehabilitation** or a **consolidation to bring it current**. This
takes months, so start it the same day you find it.

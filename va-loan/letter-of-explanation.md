# Letter of Explanation (LOE / LOX) — Draft

> Underwriters require a signed **Letter of Explanation** for tax delinquencies,
> payment plans, and any credit derogatories. This is *your* letter, in your
> voice, addressed to the lender/underwriter. Keep it factual, brief, and
> forward-looking: what happened, that it's resolved or on a plan, and proof
> attached. Do **not** over-explain or get emotional — underwriters want clean
> facts and evidence.
>
> Fill every `[BRACKET]`. Delete any section that doesn't apply to you.

[DATE]

*(Attached: filed returns and IRS/FTB transcripts confirming receipt.)*

**4. Child support.**
My child-support obligation is $[AMOUNT] per month and the account is current with

committed to keeping every obligation current through and beyond closing. Please
let me know if you need anything further.

### What the study is about

I built the Chiropractic Sleep Academy (CSA). I want to **test a new model** for
better sleep and breathing. No one has proven this model yet. That is the point
of the study — to **measure if it helps**, with real before-and-after numbers.

### The model we are testing

We do it in this order:

1. **VAET first** — Viscerocranial Airway Expansion Therapy. A hands-on,
   structural treatment. *(This is the first step in the study, not a
   replacement for any medical care.)*
2. **Then CBP + SOT for about 90 days** — chiropractic care to hold the change.
3. **Then maintenance adjustments** — to keep your **Unsubluxated Structural
   Breathing System** stable over time.

### What you would do

- Answer some questions about your sleep.
- Take a **home sleep test** (it is mailed to you; nothing to send back).
- Your test is read by a **sleep physician (MD) or a polysomnographic tech.**
  I also review it and make the clinical read within my scope as a California
  Doctor of Chiropractic.
- We go over your results together on **Zoom**.
- If it fits, we do the treatment steps above.
- We **re-test over 2 years** to see what changed.

### What we measure

Your sleep numbers, such as **AHI, RDI, ODI, oxygen levels (SpO2)**, and a
sleepiness score. We compare before and after.

## Before you use this (for Robert — NOT patient-facing)

This is a draft. Do not send it until these are done:

1. **Attorney review** — research consent, HIPAA language, data storage/BAA,
   advertising/claims, and CA DC **scope of practice** (your right to interpret
   and diagnose OSA as a CA DC — get this confirmed in writing).
2. **IRB** — get IRB approval or a written exemption **before you publish or
   present** any results (see the chat notes; a private IRB like Advarra/WCG
   works). The consent structure here is meant to be IRB-ready.
3. **Fill the blanks** — costs, risks, and any study details.
4. **HIPAA storage** — decide where signed forms + health data live (EHR /
   BAA-covered system, not a marketing tool). Keep this out of the CRM.
5. **Two tracks stay separate** — this study consent is different from the
   email opt-out/unsubscribe for marketing. Don't mix them.

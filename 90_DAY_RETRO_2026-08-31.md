into two lines that never rejoined. 32 versions accumulated across 68 branches.
For roughly three months, every working session read a partial memory missing
five whole sections — including the mentor ecosystem, the offer architecture,
and Robert's own operator profile. Work was re-derived, decisions were
re-litigated, and corrections made in June were invisible in August. Fixed
2026-08-30. See `repo-audit/2026-08-30-branch-audit.md`.

**2. Nothing was ever filed.** 67 of 68 branches sat unmerged; `main` received
nothing after June 1. Every session's output landed somewhere no other session
could see it. This is the same failure as #1, one level up.

**3. The attention trade-off has never been named.** The SDI
disability-certification business generated roughly $1,800 across eleven
payments on 8/20–8/21 alone, runs every week, and has staff reporting on it.
CSA's total first revenue was earned once, on a stage, in Serbia. Every CSA
plan to date has quietly assumed Robert's attention is free. It is not — it is
being taken from a materially larger, already-working business.

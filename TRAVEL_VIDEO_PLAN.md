### The CA EDD SDI piece — intent (confirmed)
The angle: when a **CSA patient is a W-2 employee in California** carrying financial /
mental stress (e.g. while on State Disability Insurance through CA EDD), Dr. Zeravica's
recommendation is to **make more money by building an online income** — buy a domain,
stand up a website, and sell a service or product from it.

It's an **entrepreneurship / financial-empowerment** message, not a clinical claim — so the
§5 cure/medical red lines don't apply here. Keep it credible and non-hype per §4:
practical, doctor-teacher tone; don't position it as financial/legal advice or a guaranteed
outcome; keep it clearly separate from clinical care so the two messages don't blur.

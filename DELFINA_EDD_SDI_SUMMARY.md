# 6. CA EDD SDI Concepts

Robert then shifted into his California EDD SDI practice.

He requested a concise executive summary differentiating:

* medical leave,
* work restrictions,
* disability time.

---

## Medical Leave

Defined as:

Complete removal from work.

Characteristics:

* patient should not work,
* temporarily unable to perform usual job,
* expected to recover.

Simple summary:

> No work.

---

## Work Restrictions

Defined as:

Patient can work only with limitations.

Examples include:

* lifting limits,
* sitting restrictions,
* standing restrictions,
* reduced hours,
* modified duty,
* work-from-home.

Important distinction:

If employer cannot accommodate restrictions, SDI benefits may still be appropriate.

Simple summary:

> Some work.

---

## Disability Time

Defined as:

Temporary impairment causing functional inability to perform usual work and resulting wage loss.

Important emphasis:

EDD focuses on:

**functional capacity**, not diagnosis alone.

Simple summary:

> Loss of work ability resulting in loss of income.

# 8. Doctor Certification Questions

Robert requested the non-demographic physician questions typically required when certifying disability.

The summary identified nine core physician determinations:

1. What medical condition exists?

2. What functional limitations does it produce?

3. Why can't the patient perform usual work?

4. Is the patient completely off work or restricted?

5. What specific restrictions exist?

6. When did disability begin?

7. How long is disability expected to last?

8. Is the patient under ongoing treatment?

9. Is recovery expected?

---

# 9. Core Principle Emphasized

The discussion repeatedly emphasized the concept Robert already uses in practice:

> **EDD certifies functional incapacity—not diagnoses alone.**

The physician's responsibility is to connect:

**medical condition → functional limitation → inability to perform usual work → temporary wage loss.**

This functional chain—not the diagnosis by itself—is the basis of disability certification.

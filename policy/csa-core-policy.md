# Chiropractic Sleep Academy (CSA) — Core Policy

- **Effective:** Upon Issuance
- **Authority:** Founder & Director, Chiropractic Sleep Academy
- **Applies To:** All CSA personnel, contributors, partners, faculty, contractors,
  and representatives
- **Archive:** This is the founding document of the **CSA Policy Archive**
  (`/policy`). Robert-authored, 2026-07-16.

> ⚠️ **Before this is relied on as binding:** have an attorney review it. As
> written it reads well and is protective, but it uses terms with legal weight —
> "professional standards body," "referral hub," representative obligations,
> compliance/HIPAA references. Attorney sign-off keeps it defensible (Master
> Summary Priority 1 — legal). See Editor's note at the end.

---

## I. Purpose
The Chiropractic Sleep Academy (CSA) exists to educate, standardize, and elevate
chiropractic understanding and application of sleep, airway, posture, and
biomechanical health.

CSA operates as:
- An educational academy
- A professional standards body
- A referral and authority hub

**CSA is not a medical provider. CSA does not diagnose, treat, or guarantee
outcomes.**

## II. Governing Doctrine
CSA operates under a defined Operating Doctrine grounded in **Structural
Intelligence.** All CSA decisions, structures, and actions are governed by:
- CSA Structural Laws
- Written Policy
- Defined Functional Roles
- Measured Outputs (Vital Signs)

> Policy overrides opinion. Structure overrides improvisation. Clarity overrides
> force.

## III. Authority & Intent
The Founder's intent, when written as Policy or Structural Law, is the highest
governing authority within CSA.
- No committee, vote, or consensus overrides written Policy
- Verbal intent is not binding unless written
- Policy remains in force until formally amended or retired

> CSA is an academy, not a democracy.

## IV. Structural Organization
CSA is organized by **function, not title.**
- Each function has a defined role
- Each role has a stated purpose, outputs, and accountability
- One individual may hold multiple roles
- No role may exist without written definition

Overlapping roles, undefined responsibilities, or informal authority are
prohibited. *(Roles are catalogued in `content/csa-org-hats.md`.)*

## V. Educational Boundaries
CSA provides **education only.**
- CSA does not practice medicine or chiropractic care
- Clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating practitioner
- CSA does not replace licensure, diagnosis, or patient consent
- Educational material is not individualized medical advice

All content must clearly distinguish education from clinical care.

## VI. Professional Standards
CSA maintains standards for:
- Language
- Posture and biomechanics education
- Sleep-first frameworks
- Ethical representation
- Inter-professional respect

CSA does not disparage other professions; devices, modalities, or practitioners;
or regulatory bodies.

> CSA advances clarity, not conflict.

## VII. Referrals & Network Integrity
CSA referrals are standards-based, education-informed, and integrity-driven.
- CSA does not guarantee outcomes or revenue
- Participation in CSA does not imply endorsement unless explicitly stated in
  writing

## VIII. Documentation & Compliance
**Documentation is mandatory.**
- Policies, disclaimers, curricula, and role definitions must be written
- Verbal agreements are non-binding
- Compliance protects CSA, practitioners, and the public

CSA respects scope of practice, HIPAA principles (where applicable), and state and
federal regulations.

## IX. Brand & Representation
All CSA representatives must:
- Use approved language and materials
- Represent CSA accurately
- Avoid unauthorized claims
- Preserve professional tone and integrity

Personal opinions must not be presented as CSA doctrine.

## X. Continuity
CSA is designed to function without the Founder's physical presence.
- Systems replace memory
- Roles replace dependency
- Law replaces emotion

Founder involvement is catalytic, not required for daily operation.

## XI. Policy Maintenance
- Policies are issued, revised, or retired only by the Founder
- All current policies are maintained in the CSA Policy Archive (`/policy`)
- Ignorance of policy is not an excuse for noncompliance

## XII. Final Statement
CSA exists to restore order, understanding, and integrity to the conversation
around sleep and airway health within chiropractic education. This Policy is
issued to preserve **clarity, continuity, professional trust, and long-term
impact.**

*End of Policy.*

## Editor's note (not part of the policy)

**This is a strong, protective document.** Sections V, VI, and VII encode the
CLAUDE.md §5 red lines directly (education-only, no disparaging, no outcome
guarantees) — it *hard-codes* CSA's credibility. Three things to flag:

1. **Term to reconcile — "Structural Intelligence" vs. "Systems Intelligence."**
   §II grounds the doctrine in **Structural Intelligence.** The DC white paper
   (`whitepaper-biomechanical-gap-dc-edition.md`) uses **Systems Intelligence**
   (the link uniting Universal + Innate Intelligence). Are these **the same idea
   under two names**, or two distinct concepts? Pick one governing term (or define
   both and how they relate) so the doctrine stays clean. *(Logged in
   `content/csa-coined-terms.md`.)*

2. **The education / care firewall is exactly right — keep it.** §V says CSA does
   **not** practice medicine or chiropractic. This cleanly separates **CSA the
   academy** (education) from **Dr. Z the DC** (who does read/diagnose within CA
   scope, §18). Keep those two hats legally distinct — CSA never diagnoses; Dr. Z's
   clinic does. This policy does that well; protect it.

3. **Attorney review before binding.** "Standards body," "referral hub,"
   certification, and representative obligations carry real liability — and this
   pairs with the licensing/"renting credibility" model
   (`content/strategy-renting-credibility.md`). Have counsel bless it, then it
   becomes the anchor of the Policy Archive.

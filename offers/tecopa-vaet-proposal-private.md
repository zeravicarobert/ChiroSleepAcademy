## 1. What this is

Two days at Tecopa, two sessions per day, working directly with Dr. Z.

Most sleep and airway care starts at the lungs and works up, or starts at the
teeth and works out. This starts at the **structure** — the bones of the face
and the skull, the jaw, the neck, and how they sit relative to one another —
because that structure is the space your airway has to work with.

The roof of your mouth is the floor of your nose. Where those bones sit
changes the room available for air.

## 2. What is included in the $7,000

- **Two days of direct clinical attention**, two sessions per day, with Dr. Z
- **VAET** — Viscerocranial Airway Expansion Technique. The viscerocranium is
  the facial skeleton: the maxilla, palatine, zygomatic, and related bones.
  Those are the bones that form the upper airway. Work is done manually and
  with the endonasal balloon technique (CFR-BNS)
- **Education throughout** — what was found, what it means, and what you can
  do about it once you are home
- **Treatment-day documentation** — what was done, what was observed, what
  changed
- Review of your existing sleep study and any imaging you bring, explained in
  plain language

## 5. What happens before you come

Two things need to happen first, and one of them is not optional:

1. **A conversation** — a real call about your sleep and breathing history,
   and an honest answer about whether this is likely to help you. Some people
   are not good candidates. You will be told if you are one of them.
2. **A home sleep test** — ordered through CSA's partner and read by a
   qualified reader. This is the objective baseline. Without it there is
   nothing to measure against afterward.

Where indicated, CBCT imaging as well.

**Dr. Z does not interpret your sleep study.** The qualified reader does that.
Dr. Z's job is to report what it says, explain what it means in real life, and
integrate it into the structural work.

## 6. What this is not

Said directly, because this field is full of overpromising:

- **This is not a cure for sleep apnea**, and it is not offered as one.
- Structural work **may modulate** the mechanical environment of your upper
  airway. That is an honest description of how it could help. It is not a
  guarantee of a result for you specifically.
- **Do not stop any prescribed therapy.** If you use CPAP or wear an oral
  appliance, keep using it. Only the physician or dentist who prescribed it
  can change that, and this work does not replace their judgment.
- Dr. Z works **alongside** sleep physicians, ENTs, and dentists. This adds a
  structural layer those disciplines do not cover. It does not compete with
  them, and you may well need them too.

## 7. Afterward

The two days are the intensive, not the whole arc. What typically follows is
about three months of coordinated care closer to home, then a repeat home
sleep test so the before and after can actually be compared.

CSA tracks outcomes for **two years from your first session**, de-identified,
because this model has to earn its evidence rather than assert it. Taking part
is discussed separately and consented to in writing — it is never automatic,
and declining does not change your care.

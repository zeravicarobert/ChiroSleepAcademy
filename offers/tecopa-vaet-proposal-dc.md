## 1. What this is

Two days at Tecopa, two sessions per day, working directly with Dr. Z.

This is a **test adjustment experience**, not a full case plan. You come as
the patient. You go through the structural airway work yourself before you
ever offer it to anyone in your own practice.

That order is deliberate. CSA's whole model is *heal the healer first* — a
Doctor of Chiropractic who has personally been through the assessment, the
home sleep test, and the structural work speaks about it differently than
one who has only read about it.

## 2. What is included in the $7,000

- **Two days of direct clinical attention**, two sessions per day, with Dr. Z
- **VAET** — Viscerocranial Airway Expansion Technique — and the CFR-BNS
  endonasal balloon work it builds on
- **Sleep and airway education** throughout, in a teaching format, not just
  hands-on time
- **Treatment-day documentation** — what was done, what was observed, what
  changed
- Review of your existing home sleep test and any imaging you bring, read
  through the structural airway lens

## 5. What happens before you come

Tecopa is Step 4, not Step 1. Before the trial experience you will have:

1. A **CSA Doctor Alignment Call** (free) — a real conversation about whether
   this fits you, as a patient, as a practice-builder, or both
2. A **CSA Sleep Baseline** — a home sleep test ordered through CSA's partner,
   read by the vendor's qualified reader
3. Where indicated, a **CSA Airway Baseline** — CBCT imaging and airway-focused
   review

Dr. Z reports and explains those findings and integrates them into the
structural airway pathway. **He does not interpret the sleep study** — the
polysomnographic reader does that.

## 6. What this is not

Said directly, because the sleep and airway field is full of overpromising:

- This is **not a cure** for sleep apnea, and nothing here is offered as one.
- Structural work **may modulate** the mechanical environment of the upper
  airway. That is an honest description of a mechanism, not a guarantee of an
  outcome in any individual.
- **Nobody here will tell you to stop a prescribed therapy.** If you are on
  CPAP or wearing an oral appliance, you stay on it unless the physician or
  dentist who prescribed it says otherwise.
- CSA works **alongside** sleep physicians, ENTs, and dentists. It adds a
  structural layer the other disciplines do not cover. It does not replace
  any of them.

## 7. After Tecopa

The two days are the high-bandwidth event, not the whole arc. What typically
follows is roughly three months of coordinated local care with your own
SOT- and CBP-trained provider, then a follow-up home sleep test to compare
against your baseline.

CSA tracks outcomes for **two years from the date of your first VAET session**,
de-identified. That dataset is how this model earns the right to be taken
seriously. Participation in tracking is discussed separately and consented to
in writing — it is not automatic.

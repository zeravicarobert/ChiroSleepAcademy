## Why this is in CSA scope

This isn't off-topic for Chiropractic Sleep Academy — it sits inside CSA's
clinical scope:

- **CSA scope = body + mind.** In California, a DC's scope of practice
  includes mental health. So CSA addresses both the body *and* the mind.
- **Mind → mental health → ICD-10.** ICD-10 includes a billable/codable
  mental-health condition along the lines of **mental financial stress.**
- Therefore **money, income, and assets** are clinically in scope: financial
  stress is a legitimate mental-health stressor CSA can address.
- **Money vs. currency** (an important distinction):
  - **Money** has **intrinsic** value.
  - **Currency** has **extrinsic** value.
- This is why **Bitcoin — and Silver, Gold, and other investments —**
  all fall under CSA's mind-side scope.

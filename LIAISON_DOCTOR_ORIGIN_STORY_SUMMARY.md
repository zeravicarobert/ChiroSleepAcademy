# 7. Returning Home Was Rejected

Earlier wording suggested:

children "return home."

Robert rejected this.

He explained:

That wording implies they voluntarily left.

Instead,

he views the situation more like:

The language evolved into:

Healing is possible wherever the father stands.

When children are pulled away by forces beyond their understanding,

the father remains the constant they can find again.

Robert considered this much more emotionally accurate.

### ⚠️ Strategic discipline — CPA communications are NOT privileged

This is critical. **CPA communications do NOT have attorney-client
privilege.** There is a limited federal tax-advice privilege under
**IRC § 7525**, but it has exceptions and does NOT protect:

- Communications related to tax shelters
- Communications used in criminal proceedings
- Many communications discoverable in IRS proceedings

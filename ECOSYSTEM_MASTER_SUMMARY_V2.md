## 1. Executive Summary

This project is the coordinated development of an ecosystem built around **Dr. Robert Zeravica, DC**, his established **California EDD State Disability Insurance certification practice**, his proprietary clinical work involving **VAET**, and the emerging **Chiropractic Sleep Academy™ (CSA)**.

The project has four connected—but legally, economically, and strategically distinct—parts:

1. **Protect and stabilize Dr. Z's existing CA EDD SDI business.**
2. **Build Dr. Robert Zeravica, DC as the central authority brand.**
3. **Develop VAET as a clinical, educational, and evidence-generating system.**
4. **Build CSA as a new commercial, educational, professional, and intellectual-property enterprise.**

The current strategic direction is **not** to aggressively expand the SDI business through advertising, union acquisition campaigns, or broad outbound marketing. Dr. Z wants the SDI practice to continue primarily through existing patients, repeat certifications, and organic referrals.

The main growth emphasis is now:

**Dr. Z authority brand → VAET at Tecopa → CSA → documented treatment data → sleep and airway conference positioning.**

Peter's role is intended to begin with protecting and systematizing the existing business, learning Dr. Z's actual workflows, removing administrative burdens, and then helping create new CSA assets and revenue. Peter is not seeking ownership of Dr. Z's existing SDI business or existing patient base. His contemplated economic participation is tied to **new CSA value and revenue that he materially helps create**.

## 2. Core Strategic Thesis

Dr. Z's long-term thesis is that conventional sleep-apnea care is materially incomplete because it generally emphasizes:

* Diagnostic testing
* CPAP and other equipment
* Oral appliances
* Medication where applicable
* Surgery

Dr. Z's ecosystem introduces an earlier and more structural layer:

> Sleep and airway problems may also involve cranial, cervical, mandibular, postural, neurological, respiratory-control, and whole-body biomechanical factors.

The intervention hierarchy is:

* **Early intervention:** biomechanics, structure, airway screening, breathing-system evaluation, VAET-related care and professional education
* **Mid intervention:** CPAP, oral appliances and other equipment
* **Late intervention:** medication and surgery

The intention is not necessarily to deny the role of conventional sleep medicine. It is to demonstrate that **biomechanics and structural breathing-system function are frequently absent or underrepresented in the prevailing model**.

A central developing identity is:

**Unsubluxated Structural Breathing System™**

Related conceptual language includes:

* **CSA 1.0:** Treat sleep apnea
* **CSA 2.0:** Establish an Unsubluxated Structural Breathing System™ to influence physiology
* **Expand**, rather than merely "restore"
* **Establish Structural Breathing Stability**
* Chiropractic as an early structural and neurological contribution to sleep, airway and breathing-system care

#### Developing clinical framework

Uploaded CSA educational assets show the expanding breathing-system model:

* Brainstem respiratory control in the medulla and pons
* Central and peripheral chemoreceptors
* Phrenic and hypoglossal motor pathways
* Tongue and upper-airway dilator function
* Diaphragm and rib-cage mechanics
* Abdominal pressure regulation
* Loop gain and unstable ventilatory control
* The interaction between airway mechanics and neurological respiratory drive

One CSA asset defines the medulla as the breathing-rhythm engine and the pons as the pattern shaper. This supports a broader CSA proposition:

> The breathing system is not merely the lungs, and airway collapse is not merely a tube problem. Breathing depends on the coordinated function of the controller, sensors, nerves, airway valve, tongue, craniofacial container, cervical structure, rib cage, diaphragm and abdominal pressure system.

Collect structured pre- and post-intervention data sufficient to:

* Evaluate VAET outcomes
* Identify which patients respond
* Refine the clinical protocol
* Produce case reports

* Support professional education

* Develop future research partnerships

### Potential data sources

* Home sleep testing
* AHI and related respiratory indices
* Oxygen saturation
* Snoring measures
* Sleep quality
* Patient-reported outcomes
* Mallampati observations
* Airway photography
* Posture and forward-head measurements
* Cervical findings
* TMJ and cranial findings
* CBCT where clinically and legally appropriate
* Tongue and hypoglossal-related function
* Nasal airflow observations
* Functional breathing measures
* Treatment dosage
* Follow-up durability
* Adverse events

### Scientific discipline required

CSA communications should distinguish among:

* Established anatomy and physiology
* Plausible mechanisms
* Dr. Z's clinical observations
* Working hypotheses
* Preliminary case results
* Controlled research conclusions

For example, the uploaded CSA material supports a legitimate systems-level discussion of the brainstem, chemoreceptors, phrenic nerve, hypoglossal nerve, diaphragm and airway muscles. It does not, by itself, prove that a particular chiropractic or VAET intervention changes sleep-apnea outcomes. That outcome claim must come from actual data.

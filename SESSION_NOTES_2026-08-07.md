# Session Notes — 2026-08-07

## Flash thought captured: "Fix Your Sleep for Life" — Dr. Z's 12-Month Program

**Origin:** Robert's flash thought — a named, productized **12-month
program** under Dr. Zeravica, working title **"Fix Your Sleep for Life."**
Framed as something that "can be used in conjunction with EDD temporary
disability — or not."

### The concept, sharpened

A 12-month, doctor-led structural-airway program (vs. the à la carte
step pathway in CLAUDE.md §8). Think of it as the **long-arc container** the
existing steps could live inside:

> Sleep Baseline (HST) → Airway Baseline (CBCT) → VAET trial → guided case
> plan → 12 months of structured follow-through, tracking, and re-testing.

Why a 12-month frame is strong:
1. **Sleep + airway change is slow and structural.** A year matches the
   real biology better than a one-and-done event — honest, not hype.
2. **Recurring-revenue shape.** A 12-month program is a natural high-ticket
   or monthly container (maps to §8 Step 5 / Step 6 economics).
3. **Outcome tracking over time** = the de-identified before/after data CSA
   already wants (§8 data-ownership requirement). Re-test at 3/6/12 months.
4. **"For Life" = the durable-result promise** patients actually want.

### Two brand-guardian flags (§4–§5) before this goes anywhere public

**1. The name "Fix Your Sleep for Life."**
"Fix" + "for Life" reads as a **guaranteed permanent outcome / cure claim** —
a §5 red line ("Promise cures, guaranteed outcomes"). Great internal energy,
risky on a public page. Softer public-facing framings that keep the spirit:
- "Your Sleep for Life" (aspiration, not guarantee)
- "The Sleep-for-Life Program"
- "A Year to Better Sleep & Breathing"
- "The 12-Month Structural Airway Program"
Keep "Fix Your Sleep for Life" as the internal codename; pick a compliant
public name later.

**2. The EDD temporary-disability tie-in — biggest open question.**
This is a genuine compliance minefield and I don't want to guess at what you
mean. Reasons to slow down here:
- Marketing a cash program "in conjunction with" **state disability benefits
  (CA EDD / SDI)** can look like steering patients onto disability, or
  implying the program treats a *disabling* medical condition — which edges
  into OSA diagnosis/medical claims (DCs don't diagnose OSA — §5).
- Anything that touches disability certification, wage-replacement timing, or
  "use your disability period/funds for this program" needs a clear, honest,
  legally-reviewed story before it ever appears in copy.
- **Not saying no** — a 12-month program *could* legitimately be something a
  DC-patient does during a life season when they have time. But the
  "in conjunction with EDD" language should not go public without attorney
  input (same lane as the Peter term sheet in `partnership/`).

Our Approach

A structural lens — added to your care, not instead of it

Structural Airway Chiropractic looks at the spine, cranium, posture, and airway as one connected system. Here's how we think about your sleep, and where we fit.

01

We listen and screen

Your sleep and airway history, simple questionnaires, and a structural, postural, and cranial exam — to understand the picture before anything else.

02

We add objective data

A home sleep test — ordered through us and interpreted by a licensed sleep physician — gives an objective baseline, the same way an X-ray does for the spine.

03

We coordinate care

If your results point to a medical issue, we help connect you with the right physician, dentist, or specialist — and we support the structural side of your care.

Being Honest With You

What we do — and what we don't.

Trust matters more than any claim. So here's exactly where we fit, and where your physician, dentist, and sleep specialist come first.

✓

We don't diagnose or treat sleep apnea. That's a medical diagnosis, made by physicians. We screen, add the structural picture, and refer.

✓

We don't replace CPAP, oral appliances, or your doctor. These have their place. We work alongside them, not against them.

✓

We don't promise cures. We offer an honest structural evaluation and care — and we're clear about what is proven and what is still being studied.

✓

We lead with your data. Objective testing comes before any plan, so decisions are grounded in facts, not pressure.

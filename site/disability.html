California Disability & EDD SDI help.

A separate part of my practice helps eligible California workers navigate California EDD State Disability Insurance (SDI) — the medical certification, recertification, and paperwork that come with a qualifying health condition.

Who this is for

This service is for California workers who are temporarily unable to do their job because of a qualifying health condition — and who need help filing an EDD State Disability Insurance claim correctly.

  • Union workers and boilermakers
  • Nurses and healthcare workers
  • Teachers
  • Truck drivers
  • Physical laborers
  • Other California workers with a qualifying condition

What we help with

  • Telehealth medical certification
  • Recertification and continued-claim support
  • Employer and claim forms
  • Appeal assistance where appropriate
Important. This service is completely separate from Chiropractic Sleep Academy and structural airway care. We help you file correctly and honestly. We do not guarantee approval or any specific outcome — the decision belongs to the EDD.

Common questions

Can you guarantee my claim is approved?

No. No provider can. We help you complete and support your claim correctly and honestly, but the approval decision is made by the California EDD.

Is this the same as Chiropractic Sleep Academy?

No. This is a separate disability-consulting service. Chiropractic Sleep Academy is about structural sleep and airway education and care. They are kept clearly apart.

Do I need to come in?

Much of the process can be handled through telehealth, depending on your situation.

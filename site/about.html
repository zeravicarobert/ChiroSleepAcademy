The doctor behind the method.

I'm Dr. Robert Zeravica — a chiropractor, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran, and the founder of Chiropractic Sleep Academy. For the last 13-plus years I've focused on one question: how does the structure of the head, airway, and neck shape the way we breathe and sleep?

I've been a chiropractor since 1999. For the first half of my career — my first 13 years or so — I focused on the spine: low backs, necks, and the everyday neuromusculoskeletal work chiropractors are known for.

Then my patients started telling me something I didn't expect. After an adjustment, they'd come back and say they were sleeping better than they had in years. Not one or two of them — enough that I couldn't ignore it. So I got curious about sleep, and I started to study it seriously.

When I went to the sleep and airway conferences to learn, I hit a wall. Those rooms were built for two professions — medicine and dentistry. As a chiropractor, there simply wasn't a seat for me at the table.

"There was no seat for chiropractors at the sleep table. So I built one."

That table is Chiropractic Sleep Academy

Instead of walking away, I built the place I couldn't find. Chiropractic Sleep Academy gives chiropractors a seat in the sleep and airway conversation — a place to bring the structural and biomechanical side of breathing and sleep, working alongside dentists, physicians, and sleep specialists rather than against them.

An educator, not just a clinician

I've taught continuing education for chiropractors in California more than once, and I've been approved as a CE lecturer in the state. I've also submitted an initial outline of this material to Los Angeles College of Chiropractic / Southern California University of Health Sciences for review and possible undergraduate or postgraduate education. My goal is to see this become a recognized part of how chiropractors are trained.

A note on scope. As a chiropractor, my role is structural. I don't diagnose or treat sleep apnea. I evaluate structure, coordinate testing, and work alongside dentists, physicians, and sleep specialists.

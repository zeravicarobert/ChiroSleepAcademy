## Who this site is for

**Primary audience: chiropractors (DCs).** This is the DC-facing academy /
authority site — the public face of Structural Airway Chiropractic and the
front door to the "Heal the Healer First" pathway (CLAUDE.md §7–8).

The **patient-facing** track (the EDD/SDI sleep funnel, `funnel/`) is
separate. Patients do **not** get DC-training messaging. If patient-facing
pages ever live on this domain, they sit on their own path/subsection with
their own voice — never blended into the DC nav.

**Brand guardrails apply to every page** (CLAUDE.md §4–5): doctor-teacher
voice, no cure claims, no hype, respectful of CPAP/oral appliances/surgery/
MDs/DDS/ENTs, honest about what's proven. Re-read §5 before writing copy for
any pillar — especially Pillar 3 (Sales/Closing) and Pillar 5 (Data).

## Public vs. gated content

Each pillar has a **public layer** (authority-building, SEO, converts
strangers) and a **member layer** (the real training, gated behind CSA
Doctor Membership — CLAUDE.md §8, Step 6). Start by building the public
layer; the member layer becomes the course area later.

| Pillar | Public layer | Member layer (later) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Anatomy & Physiology | Explainer articles, diagrams, intro videos | Full curriculum, deep dives |
| 2 Practice Management | Overview of the CSA clinical flow | Templates, scripts, forms |
| 3 Case Presentation | The ethical philosophy + why it works | Full frameworks, scripts, ROF decks |
| 4 Provider Network | What it is, how referrals work | Vetted directory, intro templates |
| 5 Data & Outcomes | Honest headline results, methodology | Full dataset, member benchmarking |

## Homepage structure (section by section)

1. **Hero** — the category-defining promise.
   - Headline: names the category (Structural Airway Chiropractic) and the
     transformation for the DC.
   - Subhead: who it's for (SOT/AK/CFR/CBP/posture-focused, airway-curious DCs).
   - Primary CTA button → Free Alignment Call.
2. **The shift** — why sleep & airway is the opening for chiropractic, framed
   as a credible, collaborative, first-mover clinical category (not anti-medicine).
3. **The 5 pillars** — a clean 5-card row; each card = one pillar, one line,
   links into that pillar. This section makes the whole model legible at a glance.
4. **Heal the Healer First** — the pathway snapshot (DC as patient → HST →
   ROF → VAET → rollout → training). Links to how it works.
5. **Proof** — Dr. Z's story teaser + a single honest data headline (feeds
   from Pillar 5). Credibility without hype.
6. **Offer / next step** — the pathway + pricing posture ("Free to explore,
   affordable to test, premium to implement"), primary CTA repeated.
7. **Footer** — nav, contact, brief mission line, legal/disclaimer.

## The 5 Pillars

For each pillar: **purpose**, **what lives there**, **primary CTA**, and
**red-line note**.

### Pillar 1 — Anatomy & Physiology
- **Purpose:** Establish the science and *define the category*. This is the
  credibility floor — it earns the right to everything else.
- **What lives there:** Airway anatomy, breathing physiology, skull-down /
  sleep-first structural model, the biomechanics behind VAET, how structure
  relates to sleep-disordered breathing. Articles, diagrams, short videos.
- **CTA:** "See how this becomes a practice model →" (to Pillar 2) and the
  standing Free Alignment Call.
- **Red-line note:** Explain mechanism and rationale; be explicit about what
  is established vs. still being studied. No claim that structural care cures
  OSA. DCs don't diagnose — frame the role as structural.

### Pillar 2 — Practice Management (Consult, Exam, ROFs)
- **Purpose:** Show DCs *how to run it* — the clinical workflow that replaces
  the old Consult→Exam→X-ray→ROF with the sleep-and-airway version.
- **What lives there:** The CSA clinical flow — Consult → Structural Airway
  Exam → **Home Sleep Test** (the new "X-ray") → Report of Findings → Case
  Presentation. Questionnaires (Epworth, STOP-BANG, CSA screen), exam
  checklists, HST pathway, ROF structure. Member layer holds the actual
  templates/forms.
- **CTA:** "Learn how to present and enroll the case →" (to Pillar 3).
- **Red-line note:** HST is physician-read; Dr. Z / the DC coordinate and
  interpret structurally. Keep scope honest.

### Pillar 3 — Case Presentation & Closing  *(Sales & Closing)*
- **Purpose:** Help DCs ethically present findings and enroll patients into
  care — the "ethical financial close," done right.
- **What lives there:** Case presentation frameworks, how to translate HST +
  structural findings into a clear plan, presenting options across the
  early/mid/late spectrum, ethical enrollment, handling questions, pricing
  and positioning. Member layer holds full scripts and ROF decks.
- **CTA:** Standing Free Alignment Call; "Build your referral network →"
  (to Pillar 4).
- **Red-line note (important):** This pillar must read as *ethical clinical
  enrollment*, never high-pressure sales. No hype, no fear-based tactics,
  patient interest first (CLAUDE.md §5). The public label softens "Sales &
  Closing" to "Case Presentation & Closing" to protect credibility — the
  substance still covers closing, framed as a respected clinician would.

### Pillar 4 — Provider Network (cross-referrals)
- **Purpose:** The collaboration engine — DC ↔ DDS ↔ MD ↔ ENT ↔ HST/CBCT
  partners. Referrals both ways, ethically.
- **What lives there:** What the network is, how cross-referrals work, how to
  find and vet partners, intro/referral templates, collaboration ethics. Ties
  to the real contact bench in the "CSA Relationship Strategies" sheet. Member
  layer holds the vetted directory.
- **CTA:** "Join the network / apply" → routes into the Alignment Call.
- **Red-line note:** Respectful, collaborative tone toward every profession.
  No positioning chiropractic *against* dentistry or sleep medicine.

### Pillar 5 — Data & Outcomes (for the sleep industry and the world)
- **Purpose:** Showcase honest, de-identified outcomes — the evidence that
  makes CSA credible to the sleep industry and the public. This is the
  long-game differentiator.
- **What lives there:** De-identified before/after metrics (AHI, RDI, ODI,
  SpO2, ESS) from the patient outcomes tracking, methodology, what's being
  measured and why, and honest reporting of results as they come in. Fed
  directly by `funnel/outcomes-tracker-template.csv` and the 2-year tracking.
- **CTA:** "See the model behind the data →" (to Pillar 1) and Alignment Call.
- **Red-line note (important):** Only de-identified data. Report honestly —
  including null or mixed results. No cure claims, no cherry-picking, no
  overstating significance. This pillar's power *is* its honesty; the moment
  it hypes, it loses the sleep industry.

## Build order (recommended)

Build for coherence and conversion first, depth later:

1. **Homepage** (hero + 5-pillar overview + CTA) — makes the whole model
   legible and gives one working path. Highest leverage.
2. **About Dr. Z** — credibility; reuse the real story we're writing for the
   patient email.
3. **Pillar 1 (Anatomy & Physiology)** public layer — the category/credibility floor.
4. **Pillar 2 (Practice Management)** public layer.
5. **Pillar 5 (Data)** shell — even a "here's what we're measuring, results
   coming" honest placeholder is powerful and unique.
6. Pillars 3 & 4 public layers.
7. Member/gated layers across all five (the course area) — last.

Rationale: a stranger can understand and act on the site after step 1; each
step after that deepens authority. We draft copy one page at a time so it
stays in your voice and on-brand.

The patient journey

Every step, in order.

1

Consultation

We talk through your sleep, breathing, and health history — snoring, fatigue, CPAP history, and your goals.

2

Structural airway exam

A skull-down look at face, jaw, nose, tongue, cranial structure, and head posture.

3

Home sleep test

An easy at-home test, ordered through a partner and read by a physician. This is your objective baseline — the sleep-and-airway version of an X-ray.

4

CBCT imaging

When appropriate, a 3D cone-beam image to see the airway and craniofacial structure.

5

Report of findings

We sit down and go through what we found, in plain language, and lay out your options and next steps.

6

Structural care plan

A plan that may include structural chiropractic, cranial and postural care, and VAET — often over about three months, using CBP and SOT concepts.

7

Objective reassessment

We re-measure so change is something we can see — not just something we hope for.

8

Long-term follow-up

Ongoing check-ins, sometimes over a couple of years, to track and support your structure and sleep.

What to expect from us. We're structural, and we're honest. Chiropractors do not diagnose or treat sleep apnea. We evaluate structure, coordinate testing, and work alongside your dentist, physician, or sleep specialist. No cure or specific result is promised.

Common questions

Do I stop seeing my doctor or using CPAP?

No. Keep your medical and dental care. Our work is structural and meant to fit alongside it, not replace it.

Is the home sleep test hard?

No. You take it at home, in your own bed. A physician reads the results, and we review them with you through a structural lens.

Will this cure my sleep apnea?

We don't make cure claims. We evaluate and support structure and track changes objectively, working with your care team.

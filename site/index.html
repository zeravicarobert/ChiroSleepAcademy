The Category

What is Structural Airway Chiropractic?

A skull-down, sleep-first way of thinking about the spine, cranium, and airway. It gives chiropractors a structural lens on sleep complaints — and a clear, ethical scope: not to diagnose sleep apnea, but to screen, educate, correct what is structural, and coordinate the right care.

01

Structural, not diagnostic

DCs don't diagnose obstructive sleep apnea — physicians do. Your role is the structural and postural picture, and knowing exactly when and to whom to refer.

02

Objective, not opinion

A home sleep test becomes the sleep-and-airway equivalent of the X-ray: the objective data that anchors an honest conversation with the patient.

03

Collaborative, not competitive

CPAP, oral appliances, and surgery all have their place. CSA teaches you to work with dentists, ENTs, and sleep medicine — and to be a credible voice in the room.

How We Practice

Credibility is the whole point.

CSA exists to make chiropractic a credible, ethical voice in the sleep-and-airway conversation. That means being honest about what is proven, clear about scope, and respectful of every other provider at the table. These lines don't move.

✓

We don't claim to cure sleep apnea. Chiropractic and VAET are structural care, not a cure for a medical condition.

✓

We don't diagnose OSA. DCs screen and refer; physicians diagnose. Everyone stays in scope.

✓

We respect CPAP, oral appliances, and surgery. The goal is collaboration with dentists, ENTs, and sleep medicine — never competition.

✓

We lead with objective data. Home sleep testing and imaging anchor an honest report of findings, not a hard sell.

## Notes

### Bitcoin as the evolution of money — and of computing
- Bitcoin is the **evolution of money**.
- Bitcoin is **also the evolution of computing** — not only money.

### The units of digital information
- **Bit** = 1 digit (a 1 or a 0)
- **Nibble** = 4 bits (four 1s or 0s)
- **Byte** = 8 bits (eight 1s or 0s)

### Moore's Law
- Transistor prices go **down** roughly **every 2 years**.
- From this law we can predict the future **power** of computer technology and the future **price** of technology.

## 0. First, a framing correction worth 30 seconds

ACNB and ACFN are **credentialing boards**, not vendors you hire. Neither
one sells training, and neither one will quote you a "services" price.
What you actually pay for breaks into three separate buckets, and they
bill separately:

| Bucket | Who charges it | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Education / tuition** | **Carrick Institute** | The 300–305 hour CNS curriculum that makes you *eligible* to sit |
| **Board examination** | **ACNB** (DACNB) and/or **ACFN** (Fellowships) | The right to sit the written + practical exam and hold the credential |
| **Annual maintenance** | ACNB and ACFN | Recertification / registry dues + CE each year, for life |

So "hire them" = enroll at Carrick, then pay a board to examine and
credential you. If what you actually meant is *hire a chiropractic
neurologist as CSA faculty or a clinical collaborator*, that is a
completely different conversation — individual consulting rates, not a
published fee schedule. Tell me which and I'll run the right play.

## 1. What is publicly published (verified)

### ACFN — American College of Functional Neurology
PO Box 247, Cape Canaveral, FL 32920 (same town as Carrick Institute)

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual registry registration fee | **$185.00** | Due annually by **July 1**. Applies per Fellowship/Certificate. |
| Late fee | **$100.00** | Assessed **per Fellowship/Certificate** if documents + dues aren't in on time. |
| Re-examination cycle | not published | Fellows must recertify **by examination every 10 years** — exam cost not stated publicly. |
| Initial application / exam fee | **NOT PUBLISHED** | Must be requested directly. |

**Annual maintenance requirements attached to that $185:**
- Minimum **25 hours** CE in a board-approved program
- Bibliography of **at least 24** neurology-related peer-reviewed journal articles

**Important cost multiplier:** ACFN is an umbrella over multiple specialty
boards, and the $185 + $100 late fee are charged **per credential held**:
- ABBIR — American Board of Brain Injury and Rehabilitation
- ABVR — American Board of Vestibular Rehabilitation
- ABCDD — American Board of Childhood Developmental Disorders
- ABNN — American Board of Neurochemistry & Nutrition
- ABHP — American Board of Human Performance
- ABES — American Board of Electrodiagnostic Specialties

Hold three Fellowships and the annual carry is 3 × $185 = **$555/yr**, not $185.
(ABVR is the one most directly relevant to CSA's sleep/airway/cranial work.)

### ACNB — American Chiropractic Neurology Board

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Examination fee | **NOT PUBLISHED** | Amount lives behind the candidate login only. |
| Late registration | **+20% of the registration fee** | Applied after the posted registration deadline (e.g. the 3/31 cycle deadline). |
| Annual recertification fee | **NOT PUBLISHED** | Site instructs diplomates to log into their profile to see the amount. |
| Refunds | **Non-refundable** | Exception by petition for severe illness, active military duty, or other extenuating circumstances — Board decides case-by-case, must be requested **before** the exam date. Fees may be rolled to a future exam. |

**Annual recertification requirements attached to that fee:**
- **30 hours** CE from a CAGEN-approved source
- Bibliography of **at least 24** neurology-related peer-reviewed journal articles

**Credential:** DACNB — requires 300 post-doctorate hours in functional
neurology plus passing the written **and** performance/practical exams.
ACNB is nationally accredited, which is why its fee is worth paying if you
go this route at all.

**Contact:** ExecutiveDirector@acnb.org — (254) 231-8508

### Carrick Institute (the education cost — the biggest line item)

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CNS Curriculum, full program | **NOT PUBLISHED** on public pages | Priced at enrollment. |
| Bundle discount vs. paying module-by-module | **$2,910.08 savings** | Stated publicly — implies a 13-module à-la-carte price meaningfully above the bundle. |
| Individual on-demand courses (third-party listing) | ~**$395.00** per course | Seen on cninstitute.org, not confirmed as Carrick's own list price. |

Program shape: **305 hours**, 13 modules, prepares for **both** the ACNB
Diplomate exam and the ACFN Fellowship exams. Fast Track completes in
~12–14 months; you get 36 months from purchase to finish.

## 6. What to expect on total cost

Being straight with you: the education is the dominant line item, not the
board fees. Programs of this shape in the chiropractic post-doc market
typically land in the **five figures** for tuition, with board exam fees a
comparatively small add-on and annual maintenance running a few hundred
dollars per credential per year, forever. Nobody has quoted us a number,
so treat that as an expectation to test with the three emails above — not
a figure to plan around.

Before Robert writes a check, the CSA question to answer is:
**does DACNB/ACFN credentialing move the CSA offer, or is it a credential
detour?** Structural Airway Chiropractic is a category CSA is building —
a functional-neurology diplomate adds real clinical depth and real
authority in the airway/vestibular conversation, but it is a 12–14 month,
five-figure commitment landing in the middle of a year where the 90-day
goals are a public offer, live outreach, and one working funnel.
Worth deciding deliberately, not by momentum.

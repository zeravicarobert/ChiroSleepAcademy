## ⚠️ Compliance guardrails (read before first send)

These are your own **established patients**, which is what makes re-contact
legitimate. Keep it that way:

1. **California CMIA + HIPAA** — frame every email as a *wellness / patient
   re-engagement* invite. **Never** reference a patient's disability claim,
   SDI status, diagnosis, or specific medical history in the email.
2. **CAN-SPAM** — every email must include: a truthful from-name and subject,
   a real physical mailing address, and a working **one-click unsubscribe**.
   Honor opt-outs within 10 days; keep a suppression list.
3. **No cure claims** — never state or imply that chiropractic/VAET cures
   sleep apnea, and never imply you diagnose OSA. The role is structural
   screening + objective data + appropriate referral.
4. **Respectful** — no attacks on CPAP, oral appliances, MDs, or dentists.

**Standard footer (paste on every email):**

> Dr. Robert Zeravica · [Clinic Name] · [Street, City, CA ZIP]
> You're receiving this because you're a patient of our practice.
> [Unsubscribe] — we'll honor it right away.

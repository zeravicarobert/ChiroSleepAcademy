## Before you send (for Robert — not patient-facing)

1. This is the **invite**. The **consent form** (`funnel/patient-consent-optin-
   sleep-study.md`) is what they sign to actually join. Send the form after they
   say yes, or link it.
2. Get the **attorney + IRB** review done first (same as the consent form).
3. Fill: phone, email, website, and the form link.
4. Send from one inbox and keep replies there.
5. Log each patient in the **CSA Patient Tracker (Master)** sheet as you go.
6. Keep health data (test results) out of the email tool — EHR/BAA only.

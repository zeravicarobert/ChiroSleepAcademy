---

## Guardrails for this specific thread

- **Keep the collateral general and educational.** Nothing sent here should
  reference *this* patient's condition, symptoms, work status, work capacity,
  or restrictions. The one-pager is about the services, not about them.
- **Do not let the referral thread become a claim-documentation channel.** If
  they ask for anything touching the disability claim — a note, a letter, work
  restrictions, an opinion on ability to work — take that out of the referral
  conversation and handle it separately in the clinical record, in writing,
  through the normal process.
- **Scope line stays in, every time:** chiropractors do not diagnose or treat
  obstructive sleep apnea. A physician reads and diagnoses the home sleep test.
  The role here is structural, and collaborative with their doctor or dentist.
- **No cure claims, no CPAP-bashing.** Some of Domingo's people are probably on
  CPAP already and struggling with it. The message is "I add the structural
  layer," never "you won't need that machine."
- **Referral etiquette:** thank them, but don't turn a patient into an unpaid
  salesperson. Ask for nothing beyond passing the sheet along.

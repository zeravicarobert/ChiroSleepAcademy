someone else, usually a dental practice. Solving it looks like a vetted
national referral network of large-FOV CBCT sites that will scan a CSA patient
to protocol. This is an access and relationship problem, not a clinical one.

## Exam sequence referenced above (for reuse in clinical content)

Face and skull posture assessment, in order:
frontal bone (L/R height) → zygomatic bones → mastoid processes → external
occipital protuberance → earlobes → TMJ motion on opening/closing → Mallampati
oral cavity → palate vault (high vs. smooth) → unilateral nasal patency → eye
range of motion → intraoral soft-tissue stretch (diagonal facial pulls away
from the nose) → medial and lateral pterygoid palpation → tongue inspection →
dental hygiene assessment → standing posture (forward head posture, loss of
cervical lordosis) → vertebral subluxation → SOT pelvic category (1/2/3) →
bilateral navicular drop test.

Interpretation: distinguishes **ascending** patterns (originating at the feet)
from **descending** patterns (originating at the face), with the TMJ–SIJ Lovett
brother relationship as a key link, and names the structural nuances that call
for early cranial and spinal intervention.

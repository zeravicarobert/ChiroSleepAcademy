**Re:** Request for written guidance — scope of practice under 16 CCR §302
(home sleep testing and diagnosis of structural upper-airway obstruction)

Dear Board of Chiropractic Examiners,

I am a California-licensed doctor of chiropractic (license
#26527) seeking written guidance on the scope of practice under
16 CCR §302 as it applies to sleep-and-airway evaluation in a cash-based
practice.

My clinical focus is the **structural and anatomical contributors to
upper-airway obstruction** — cranial, facial, cervical, and postural
factors that affect the airway. As part of a structural evaluation, I
would like to understand how §302 applies to the following:

1. **Home sleep testing.** May a licensed California chiropractor order,
   provide, or administer a home sleep test (HST) — a device recording
   respiratory airflow, respiratory effort, and blood-oxygen saturation —
   for the purpose of objectively evaluating a patient's airway function?

2. **Interpretation and diagnosis.** May a chiropractor interpret the
   objective data from such a test (e.g., apnea-hypopnea index, oxygen
   desaturation index) and, consistent with chiropractic methods, render a
   diagnosis of a **structural upper-airway obstruction** — as distinct
   from rendering a medical diagnosis of "obstructive sleep apnea"?

3. **The boundary line.** Where does §302 draw the line between (a) a
   chiropractic diagnosis of a structural/anatomical airway obstruction
   and (b) the practice of medicine? What documentation or terminology
   keeps this evaluation within chiropractic scope?

4. **Referral duties.** For findings suggesting a severe or medically
   urgent condition, what referral or co-management obligations does the
   Board consider consistent with the standard of care?

I want to ensure my practice stays squarely within the lawful scope of
chiropractic in California. A written response, or a citation to any Board
guidance, advisory, or prior determination on these questions, would be
greatly appreciated.

Thank you for your time and guidance.

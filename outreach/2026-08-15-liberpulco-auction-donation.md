## 1. The Recommendation

**Donate: the CSA Structural Airway Sleep Consultation. Stated value $500.**

Not the Sleep Baseline, not a CBCT, not a Tecopa trial. Here's the reasoning.

**Why this item:**

- **It costs nothing but your time.** No HST vendor fee, no imaging partner, no
  travel. Roughly 2.5–3 hours of your time plus a written summary. Everything
  else on the ladder has real money attached — the Sleep Baseline alone carries
  a ~$189 Sleep Doctor cash-pay cost that you'd be eating for a stranger.
- **It doesn't wreck your price anchor.** It sits at the same $500 tier as the
  Airway Baseline, so the stated value is honest and consistent with what CSA
  actually charges. You're not publicly discounting a step in your own ladder.
- **It's deliverable to anyone, anywhere.** Zoom-based. The winner could be
  across the country and it still works.
- **It's the right depth for a lay crowd.** A general audience doesn't want
  doctor training — they want an answer about their own snoring, their own
  exhaustion, their own spouse's breathing at night. This gives them that.
- **It's the top of your funnel with the friction removed.** The winner enters
  the exact pathway a paying patient enters. If findings warrant more, the next
  steps are already priced.

**Why not the free Alignment Call:** it's already free. An auction item has to
be something people can't simply ask you for. This one is substantively more —
intake, tracking, a full screen, and a written summary.

**Why not the HST / Sleep Baseline:** real vendor cost out of your pocket, and
it hands a clinical test to an unscreened winner who may or may not be an
appropriate candidate. Wrong item for an auction.

| Component | Detail |

| Follow-up call | 30 minutes to walk through the summary and answer questions |

**Total: ~2.5–3 hours of direct doctor time. Stated value $500.**

### Not included (state this plainly — do not let it be assumed)

- Home sleep test (HST) or any sleep study
- CBCT or any imaging
- Hands-on treatment, adjustments, or VAET care
- Ongoing care, case management, or a treatment plan
- Any medical, dental, or surgical service
- Lab work, supplements, or appliances

If the consultation points toward any of the above, those are separate
conversations at CSA's normal rates — or a referral out. Say so in the room.

## 3. Compliance Guardrails — Read Before Writing Any Copy

This is the part that matters most. The winner is a member of the public, not a
screened DC, and they may well have undiagnosed sleep apnea. Every piece of
copy has to hold these lines:

1. **No diagnosis language.** This is a structural and educational
   consultation. Chiropractors do not diagnose obstructive sleep apnea. Never
   imply otherwise.
2. **No treatment or cure claims.** Not for sleep apnea, not for snoring, not
   for anything. No "fix," no "resolve," no "eliminate."
3. **Not a substitute for medical care.** Say this in the listing, on the
   certificate, and out loud during the consult.
4. **Referral is a feature, not a failure.** If the screen suggests meaningful
   risk, the correct outcome is a referral to a physician or sleep specialist.
   Putting that in the auction copy *builds* credibility with a lay crowd — it
   signals you're a real clinician, not someone selling from a stage.
5. **No positioning against CPAP, oral appliances, dentists, or sleep
   medicine.** Collaborative framing only.

## 7. The Part That Actually Pays You Back

The consultation is the donation. **The under-bidders are the return.**

1. **Offer every losing bidder a free CSA Alignment Call.** Step 1 of the
   pathway is free anyway, so this costs you nothing and turns a room of people
   who *raised their hand about sleep* into real conversations. This is the
   single highest-leverage move in the whole thing. Ask the organizer for it
   explicitly (it's in the email above).
2. **Get a one-pager next to the item.** A silent-auction table gives you a
   physical surface in front of an interested audience. Use it — what
   Structural Airway Chiropractic is, who it's for, where to learn more.
3. **Get the winner's contact info directly**, not just a certificate handed
   off into the void. Fulfillment is your first real touch.
4. **Offer a second unit if bidding runs hot.** If two people are fighting over
   it, a second consultation costs you nothing and doubles the goodwill. Tell
   the organizer they can take you up on that on the night.
5. **Follow up within 48 hours** of the event, while it's still warm.

I'm a California-licensed Doctor of Chiropractic and a ChiroSecure
policyholder [policy # ________]. I'm developing a cash-based,
structure-first sleep-and-airway focus in my practice and want to confirm
both the scope footing and my coverage **before** I see patients this way.

Here is exactly what I intend to do:

- Perform a **structural airway evaluation** — cranial, facial, cervical,
  and postural assessment of the anatomy affecting the upper airway.
- Use a **home sleep test (HST)** — a non-invasive device recording
  airflow, respiratory effort, and blood-oxygen saturation — as an
  objective screening tool.
- **Interpret the objective data** (AHI, RDI, ODI) and, consistent with
  chiropractic methods, document a **structural upper-airway obstruction
  (SAO)** — an anatomical finding — as distinct from rendering a medical
  diagnosis of "obstructive sleep apnea."
- **Refer and co-manage** severe or medically urgent cases with the
  appropriate physician per a written referral protocol.

My questions:

1. **Scope footing.** In ChiroSecure's risk view, is ordering/using an HST
   and documenting a structural upper-airway obstruction within the CA
   chiropractic scope of practice? Any guidance, restrictions, or
   cautions you'd flag?

2. **Coverage.** Would my current professional-liability policy **cover**
   me performing HST-based structural airway evaluation and rendering a
   structural (non-medical) SAO assessment? Is there any endorsement,
   rider, or disclosure I need for this to be covered?

3. **Documentation & consent.** What consent language and clinical
   documentation would you want to see to keep this defensible (e.g., a
   clear statement that CSA provides structural evaluation and that
   medical diagnosis/treatment is provided by referring physicians)?

4. **Referral protocol.** Does my referral/co-management plan for severe
   cases meet your expectations for standard of care?

I want to build this correctly and stay both in-scope and covered. A
written response would be greatly appreciated.

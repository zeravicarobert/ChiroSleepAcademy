# SMS Template — Free Sleep Screening

**Merge field:** `{FirstName}`
**Send from:** a HIPAA-appropriate texting platform (with opt-out handling),
not a personal phone.

---

## Initial text

> Hi {FirstName}, it's Dr. Zeravica's office. I've added sleep & airway care to
> the practice — many patients with pain or stress also have an undiagnosed
> sleep issue that makes things worse. Want a FREE 2-min sleep screening?
> Reply YES and I'll send it. Reply STOP to opt out.

## If they reply YES

> Great. Here's the quick screening: [LINK]. It takes about 2 minutes. Once you
> finish, I'll review it and let you know if a closer look makes sense. — Dr. Z's
> office

## Follow-up (5–7 days, non-responders — send once)

> Hi {FirstName}, just circling back from Dr. Zeravica's office about the free
> sleep screening. Still happy to send it if you'd like — reply YES. No worries
> if not. Reply STOP to opt out.

---

**Keep it under 320 characters. Always include the opt-out on the first send.**

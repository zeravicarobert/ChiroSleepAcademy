# Phone Script — Personal Calls (Warm Tier)

For the **164 recertified + 19 new-claim** patients. Warm, unhurried, doctor to
patient. Log every attempt in the tracker (even no-answers).

---

## Voicemail (if no answer)

> Hi {FirstName}, this is Dr. Zeravica. Nothing's wrong — I'm reaching out
> because I've added sleep and airway care to the practice, and I'd like to offer
> you a quick free sleep screening. Give the office a call back at [PHONE] when
> you get a chance. Take care.

---

## Live call

**Open:**
> Hi {FirstName}, it's Dr. Zeravica. Do you have a quick minute? Nothing's
> wrong — I wanted to reach out personally about something.

**The why:**
> A lot of the patients I treat for pain and stress are also dealing with poor
> sleep — snoring, fatigue, not feeling rested. When sleep is off, it makes pain
> and stress harder to recover from. So I've added a sleep and airway evaluation
> to the practice.

**The offer:**
> I'd like to offer you a free, quick sleep screening — just a couple of minutes
> — to see whether it's worth a closer look for you. No cost, no pressure.

**The ask:**
> Can I send you the screening by text or email, or would you rather do it right
> now with me on the phone?

**If positive screen / interested:**
> Based on this, I'd suggest the next step — a simple at-home sleep test you do
> in your own bed. I can walk you through it. Want me to get that set up?

**Close:**
> Thanks, {FirstName}. I just don't want the people I care for to miss something
> this important. We'll be in touch.

---

## Guardrails
- Don't reference their specific claim or disability.
- No promises of cures or outcomes — this is a screening and evaluation.
- If they decline, thank them and log it. One follow-up max.

## Guardrails for this thread (CLAUDE.md §5)

  Robert does not diagnose OSA; the role is structural.

  Casual channel, same red lines.

  will often lead with medical-skeptic framing; do not match it. Stay the
  clinician who collaborates.
- **Keep the politics and the medicine separate.** The passport, e-Citizenship,
  and Liberland conversation is friendship and rapport. The health offer stands
  on clinical credibility, not on shared politics.
- **Don't over-spiritualize.** "Electromagnetic" in the Triad stays clinical —
  nervous-system tone, sleep environment, stress physiology.

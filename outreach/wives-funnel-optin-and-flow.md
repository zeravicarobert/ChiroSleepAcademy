## ⚠️ Guardrails

**1. THE REASSURE LINE — rewrite before publishing (red line §5 + §18).**
Robert's draft: *"There are approaches that don't involve surgery, drugs, or
sleeping attached to equipment."* This is the **retired** "treat without drugs or
machines / VAET-instead-of-CPAP" framing (CLAUDE.md §18) — it positions CSA as an
*alternative to* medical care and knocks CPAP/surgery. Do not ship it.
> **Compliant rewrite:** *"There's also a structural side to the airway that's
> often never looked at — one that works alongside whatever care he's already
> getting."*
Keeps the hope, drops the "instead of devices/drugs/surgery" claim. Never tell a
reader to avoid or replace prescribed care.

**2. "masks often fail" (subhead) — soften.** CPAP helps millions; "masks fail"
reads anti-device. Try: *"Why willpower and equipment alone don't always solve it —
and what else to look at."*

**3. "Why Many Men Reject CPAP" (mini-guide) — keep, but frame respectfully.** It's
true many struggle with CPAP; the guide should explain *why some can't tolerate it
and what that reveals about the airway* — never "CPAP is bad."

**4. "Not a Lifestyle Problem" (mini-guide) — don't overstate.** Weight/lifestyle
*do* drive many cases. Frame as *"not only a lifestyle problem"* (consistent with
the social-post fix).

**5. $7 = a new low-ticket product.** Fits the "affordable to test" tier (CLAUDE.md
§4). Needs: the actual guide file, a checkout, and CAN-SPAM-compliant email (opt-out).

**6. Still requires** (same as the whole patient track): Robert's real story where
used, and a working email tool/list. No PHI collected on the opt-in (email + name
only).

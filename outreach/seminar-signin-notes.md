# Seminar Sign-In Sheet — how to use it

**Robert-authored, 2026-07-16.** Lead-capture for the DC seminar (pairs with the
Foundations Workbook). Print-ready files: `outreach/CSA-Seminar-Sign-In.pdf` and
`.html`.

## In the room
- Put it near the entrance or on the back table.
- Mention it **once**, casually: *"There's a sign-in sheet if you want the
  follow-up materials or references."*
- **Don't hover. Let it work.**

## What I added to your version
- A small **Event / Location + Date** line at the top — so each sheet is tagged to
  the right seminar when you enter the leads.
- **"You can opt out anytime"** to the consent line (keeps follow-up email clean
  and compliant).

## Where the data goes (keep it in order)
- These are **DC contacts** (marketing/CRM data), **not patient PHI** — so they go
  in your **DC outreach list / CRM**, not the patient tracker.
- After each seminar: enter every row, tag it with the event, and mark the top
  interest so follow-up is targeted (sleep vs. patient-first vs. consulting).
- The **"Being a patient first of Dr. Zeravica"** and **"Private Consulting"**
  checkboxes are your hottest leads — follow those up first (they map to the
  "Heal the Healer First" pathway).

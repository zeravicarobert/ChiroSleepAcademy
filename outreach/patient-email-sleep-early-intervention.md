Hi [First Name],

I want to tell you about something I've been working on, because it may
matter more to your health than almost anything else we've worked on
together — your sleep.

**Most people don't think about their sleep until it becomes a big
problem.** They snore for years. They wake up tired for years. They're
told "that's just getting older." And by the time anyone takes it
seriously, the options have narrowed.

Here's how I've come to think about it. Care for sleep and breathing
happens on a spectrum:

- **Early** — understanding what's actually happening while you sleep,
  and supporting your structure, posture, and airway before things
  progress.
- **Middle** — devices like CPAP or a dental oral appliance.
- **Later** — medications or surgery.

Every one of these has its place, and for many people the middle and
later options are exactly right and even life-saving. I'm not against any
of them. But here's the part most people miss: **the earlier you
understand your sleep, the more options you have.** You can't make a good
decision about something you've never measured.

That's the piece I focus on — the early, structural side, and helping you
get real information instead of guessing.

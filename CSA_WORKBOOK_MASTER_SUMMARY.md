## Primary Objective

This conversation developed the foundational structure, positioning, branding, curriculum, marketing ecosystem, and publishing plan for the **Chiro Sleep Academy™ Workbook (2025 Edition)**.

The long-term vision is for this workbook to become the foundational textbook for the Chiro Sleep Academy™, eventually supporting:

* Continuing Education (CE)
* Chiropractic college relationships
* Certification programs
* Online courses
* Membership communities
* Position papers
* Books
* Research publications
* Lifestyle Academy™
* A broader Chiropractic sleep ecosystem

The workbook is intended primarily for **Doctors of Chiropractic**, not patients.

# Educational Model

The workbook is modeled after educational organizations including:

* American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM)
* American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine (AADSM)
* World Dentofacial Sleep Society
* American Board of Craniofacial Dental Sleep Medicine

The discussion emphasized creating a Chiropractic equivalent to these educational systems rather than copying them.

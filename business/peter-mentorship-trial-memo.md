# Mentorship Trial Memo

> One page. Both parties read, agree, sign. Filed in repo + each
> party keeps a copy. **This is not an employment contract.** It is
> a written declaration of the mentorship-trial arrangement Peter
> and Robert agreed to verbally on 6/8/26 in San Diego, CA.

---

## Parties

- **Mentor / Operator:** Dr. Robert Zeravica, DC ("Dr. Rob")
- **Apprentice / Student:** Peter \[full legal name to be inserted
  before signing\] ("Peter")

## Start date

**June 8, 2026**, San Diego, CA. No fixed end date — trial period.

## The arrangement (in Peter's own words, 6/8/26)

> *"I don't need compensation now, other than proximity. I extract
> value from learning from Dr. Zeravica DC and the work that is
> being asked of me. Tedious is pretty simple for me and fun and
> helps me learn. I see it as a mentorship. I want compensation
> when I can generate income for Dr. Rob. At that point in time,
> we will discuss compensation changes beyond proximity. Dr. Rob
> can terminate the relationship at any time he sees it's not
> working out, because it's like a trial period. That's why I don't
> want to pay. We're still learning how to work with each other and
> what the relationship could be."*

## What Peter does

Peter shadows and supports Dr. Rob across his businesses
(Chiropractic Sleep Academy; CA EDD SDI Telehealth practice;
Northridge Diversified DC practice; VAET / Tecopa) for the purpose
of his own learning. Scope detailed in
`business/peter-director-of-operations.md`. The work is *real* —
Peter coordinates content production, drafts outreach, manages
project schedules, builds operational systems (Asana, CRM,
workflows), and handles administrative coordination. The work is
educational *for Peter*; that's the value he receives.

## What Peter does NOT do

- Make clinical decisions about specific patients
- Touch protected health information (PHI) without a signed
  Business Associate Agreement
- Make final pricing decisions on Dr. Rob's services
- Provide legal advice
- Access Dr. Rob's personal finances
- Make decisions involving Dr. Rob's family or children
- Hold himself out as Dr. Rob's employee or contractor to third
  parties

## What Peter receives in lieu of cash compensation

1. **Proximity** to a 26-year DC operator running multiple business
   lines in real time
2. **Mentorship** in clinical, business, marketing, and operational
   domains
3. **Access** to Dr. Rob's mentor stack (Mandossian, Severino,
   Abraham, Brunson programs, Mike Barron) by association, where
   Dr. Rob extends an introduction
4. **Reasonable shared meals and lodging** while Peter is
   physically with Dr. Rob's crew (e.g., the San Diego AirBnB) —
   this is **not** wage; it is the cost of having Peter physically
   present
5. **Trial-period experience** that may convert to a compensated
   role if the income trigger fires (see below)

## Classification (the important part)

This is a **mentorship arrangement**, not employment, not
independent-contractor work, and not volunteer labor for a
charitable purpose. **No employer-employee relationship exists.**
Peter is here for his own learning and freely terminates whenever
he wishes; Dr. Rob terminates whenever he wishes. Neither party owes
the other money, benefits, or post-termination obligations beyond
the confidentiality covenants below.

Either party may, at any time, consult an attorney to confirm this
arrangement complies with applicable law. If either is advised to
change the arrangement, this memo will be revised.

## Income trigger (when compensation conversation reopens)

Compensation is deferred, not waived. When any of the following
fires, the parties reopen the compensation conversation:

1. **Direct revenue attribution:** Peter sources and closes a
   patient, DC mentee, referral, or vendor deal that produces
   **$10,000 or more in attributable revenue** to Dr. Rob's
   businesses.
2. **Operational margin:** Peter's systems work measurably saves
   Dr. Rob **20+ hours/month** of his own time, sustained over 3
   consecutive months.
3. **Milestone completion:** Peter ships a defined operational
   milestone (functional Asana with all matrix items + crew tasks;
   functional CRM with the 28-DC warm database imported and live;
   the daily report cycle running on automation).

When a trigger fires, the parties agree to convene within 14 days
and define a compensation structure for the work going forward. This
memo does NOT pre-commit to any specific structure — that's the
conversation that happens at the trigger.

## Termination

**Either party may terminate this arrangement at any time, for any
reason or no reason, with no notice required and no further
obligation between them.** Peter is a trial mentorship student; Dr.
Rob is a trial mentor. If it stops working, it stops. The friendship
predates this memo and is expected to outlive it regardless of
outcome.

## Confidentiality

Peter will not disclose, during or after this arrangement, any of
the following to anyone outside Dr. Rob's authorized circle:

- Patient identities, clinical details, or PHI
- Dr. Rob's financial position, tax matters, legal exposure, or
  family situation
- Mentor relationships (Mandossian, Severino, Abraham, Barron,
  Brunson programs) where introduction has not been publicly made
- Internal strategy documents, pricing models, or unannounced
  offerings
- Communications with third parties conducted on Dr. Rob's behalf

Confidentiality survives termination indefinitely.

## What this memo does NOT do

- It does not promise Peter future employment, equity, or
  compensation
- It does not promise Dr. Rob any minimum hours, performance, or
  outcome
- It does not change either party's other commitments,
  relationships, or freedoms
- It does not create any third-party rights
